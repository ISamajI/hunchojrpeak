      The family

      Since 1936, from Hamilton Avenue

      Four generations of bread, told in seven stops. Scroll to walk through it.

      1936

      Pasquale Gervasio opens on Hamilton Avenue

      His bread earns a reputation fast. He runs the shop with his wife, Margaret, and their five children: Theresa, Phyllis, Frank, Johnny and Victor.

      1956

      The family incorporates on April 1

      The kids clean, mix, mold and deliver, building a wholesale and retail bakery in Chambersburg, Trenton's Italian neighborhood. Deliveries reach delis, supermarkets, restaurants, hospitals and homes.

      ’60s

      Pastry joins the bread, then a deli

      When a neighborhood competitor closes, the store moves and adds a pastry case. Customers ask for a deli, so the family adds one.

      1976

      Following the neighbors to the suburbs

      Between July 1976 and May 1990 the company opens eight satellite stores. Every loaf is still made at the main bakery to keep the quality the same.

      1986

      A real sourdough rye

      The bakery buys Frey's Baking Company to make a true sourdough rye. It's still on the shelf, and still buy one, get one free on Wednesdays.

      1990

      New Colonial Bakery joins next door

      The two buildings are linked with a conveyor so fresh bread rolls straight to a much bigger packing and delivery floor.

      Today

      Three bakeries, open every day

      Trenton, Mercerville and Levittown, plus more than 450 supermarkets, restaurants and organizations served daily.
