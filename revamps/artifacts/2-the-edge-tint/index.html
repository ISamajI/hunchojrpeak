Good to know

Questions

How long does a tint job take?

Most cars are in and out the same day — a few hours for a full car. We’ll give you a firm time when you book. Keep the tinted windows rolled up for a few days while the film cures.

What’s the difference between carbon and ceramic film?

Both look great and block UV. Ceramic rejects a lot more heat and won’t interfere with phone or GPS signal, so the cabin stays cooler in the summer. IR ceramic goes further on infrared heat. Try each in the Tint Lab above.

Can I get my front windows tinted in New Jersey?

Not without a medical exemption — NJ doesn’t allow aftermarket film on the windshield or front side windows otherwise. Rear side windows and back glass can go any shade. Ask us about the exemption process.

Do you wrap commercial vans and trucks?

Yes — color changes, partial wraps, lettering and chrome deletes. Book a drop-off and note what you have in mind, or send a photo to 609-297-4245.

Do I need to pay when I book?

No. Booking holds your time slot; you pay at the shop when the work is done.
