03 · About

The short version

Started acting in his forties after a career in sales. Now he’s a series lead.

Chris grew up in Brooklyn and came to acting later than most. He started with a small part in a National Lampoon feature, then joined Netflix’s Lilyhammer with Steven Van Zandt. Network roles on The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel, The Blacklist: Redemption and FBI: Most Wanted followed.

He spent 16 episodes on ABC’s For Life, produced by Curtis “50 Cent” Jackson, and plays Gaetano in Gravesend on Prime Video. He trained in stunts with coordinator John “Johnny C.” Cenatiempo, and he is now writing and starring in his own pilot.
