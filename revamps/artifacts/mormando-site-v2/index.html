The story

Brooklyn born and raised. Husband, father of two. He started acting in his early forties — and kept climbing the call sheet.

It started with a small part in a National Lampoon feature. Then Tommy Googootz on Netflix’s Lilyhammer (2013), The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel, The Blacklist: Redemption and FBI: Most Wanted — up to 16 of 23 episodes of ABC’s For Life and Gaetano across 19 episodes of Amazon’s Gravesend.

Stunts Trained, not just cast

Trained in stunts under coordinator John “Johnny C.” Cenatiempo. Stunts on Babygirl (2024) and No Hard Feelings (2023); stunt driver on The Report (2019).
