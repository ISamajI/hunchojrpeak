Two words. The whole country.

In Costa Rica, pura vida means hello, goodbye, thank you, no worries and life is good. Ticos say it all day.

At Pura Vida in Trenton it also means a plate of gallo pinto with a fried egg on top, a hot aguadulce, and a picada for the table.
