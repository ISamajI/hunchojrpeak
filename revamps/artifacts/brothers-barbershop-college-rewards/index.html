Program terms
  1. Open to students currently enrolled at an accredited college or university. A barber checks a valid student ID before your card earns points. Brothers Barbershop may ask to see it again each semester.
  2. Earn 10 points for each completed, paid haircut at Brothers Barbershop, with any barber. Limit one haircut credit per day.
  3. Canceled appointments, no-shows and unpaid services earn no points.
  4. 100 points = $5 off. 200 points = $10 off. Ask your barber before you pay. One reward per visit.
  5. Rewards have no cash value, can't be combined with other discounts or promotions, and can't be transferred or shared.
  6. We keep your name, phone, college and college email to run the program. We don't keep copies of your ID.
  7. Brothers Barbershop may change or end the program. Changes will be posted in the shop.
