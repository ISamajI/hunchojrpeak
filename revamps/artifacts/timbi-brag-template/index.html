Right where downtown Trenton begins, Timbí brings Dominican and Latin flavor to South Broad Street: chimis, yaroas, mofongo, birria and our oven-roasted chicharrón. Everybody fries it. Timbí roasts it. The skin blisters and cracks like glass, the meat stays tender, and it's the reason people drive across Mercer County. We believe Trenton's best days are still ahead, and we're proud to grow with it. Cultura · Familia · Comunidad.
