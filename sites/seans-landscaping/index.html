Who we are

The landscape partner general contractors call back.

Sean's Landscaping is a full-service commercial landscape company. We work closely with owners, contractors, developers and landscape architects to turn drawings into finished, thriving landscapes.

  • Owner on site. Our president, Gerry Brady, works side-by-side with the crews on every job.
  • Self-performed. In-house hardscape craftsmen, irrigation and green-roof crews — no hand-offs.
  • Certified & union. Roof Meadows, American Hydrotech and LiveRoof certified; signatory to six trade locals.

How we work

From bid invitation to close-out.

A simple, predictable process that keeps general contractors and owners informed at every step.

  1. Bid & estimate

    Our lead estimator prices every part of the scope from your plans and specs — green roofs, hardscape, planting and irrigation.

  2. Plan & procure

    We line up plant material, soils and stone from long-standing suppliers, including contract growing when needed.

  3. Build to spec

    A senior project manager and our in-house crews install the work exactly per the drawings, with the owner on site.

  4. Close-out & aftercare

    Punch lists, warranties and a dedicated aftercare lead so the landscape keeps flourishing after hand-over.
