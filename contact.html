FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers for contractors and owners.

What areas do you serve?

New Jersey, New York, Delaware and Eastern Pennsylvania, including the City of Philadelphia. Our office is in Hamilton, NJ (Mercer County).

Do you work as a subcontractor to general contractors?

Yes — most of our work is for general contractors and construction managers such as Turner, L.F. Driscoll, Skanska, Hunter Roberts and Barr & Barr, as well as directly for owners like RWJ Hospital and Princeton University.

Are you licensed, insured and bonded?

Yes. We are a registered NJ public-works contractor, licensed in NJ, PA, DE and Philadelphia, with surety through Allegheny Casualty Co. (through KOG International). See the full list on our qualifications page.

Are you a union contractor?

We are signatory to Operating Engineers Locals 825 and 542, Laborers Locals 172, 472 and 332, Building Trades Local 77 and Bricklayers Local 1.

Are you a certified green-roof installer?

Yes — we are certified Roof Meadows, American Hydrotech and LiveRoof contractors and have built intensive and extensive roofs from 4th-floor decks to an 80,000 sq ft hospital roof.

Do you take residential projects?

We focus on commercial, institutional and public landscape construction. Give the office a call and we'll let you know if we're a fit.

Have a project out to bid?

Send plans and specs and our estimating team will price every part of it — from green roofs to ground-level landscapes.
