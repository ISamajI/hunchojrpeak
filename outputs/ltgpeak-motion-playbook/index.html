LTGPeak Motion Playbook
LTGPeak · field manual · Oct 2026

Motion that sells a stay

Cinematic techniques, modern interactions and edit styles that award-winning hotel, villa and studio sites are shipping right now. Each one comes with a working demo, the stack to build it, and where it fits on a BnB or client site.

GSAP is free

All plugins, including ScrollTrigger, SplitText, DrawSVG and MorphSVG, cost nothing now. That removes the old reason to fake scroll scenes.

CSS scrolls itself

animation-timeline: scroll() and view() work in Chrome, Firefox and Safari 26. Simple reveals no longer need JavaScript.

INP judges motion

Google's 200ms responsiveness bar fails on a large share of sites. Animate transform and opacity only, and lazy-load the heavy stuff.

Scrubbed scrollytelling

Progress is tied to the scrollbar, so the visitor controls the playback. Apple's product pages scrub a frame sequence this way. For a house, scrub a drone flyover from the driveway to the pool.

  • StackGSAP ScrollTrigger pin: true, scrub: 1, drawing a JPG/WebP frame sequence to <canvas>
  • FramesExport 120–180 frames at 1600px WebP from the drone clip with ffmpeg. Preload the first 20.
  • SmoothLenis with lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update) on GSAP's ticker

Part three

Edit styles for the footage itself

Web video gets treated like film: short, graded, and cut on motion. These are the edits to ask a videographer for, or to cut in CapCut or Premiere yourself.

Match-cut on movement

A door opens in the hallway, and the cut lands mid-swing on the bedroom door. Rooms feel connected.

Speed ramp

A 120fps gimbal walk slowed to 40%, then snapped back to real time on the reveal.

Golden-hour grade

One LUT across every clip, warm highlights and teal shadows, so stills, loops and splats all match.

Seamless loop point

Cut where the end frame matches the start frame, or crossfade 12 frames, so the hero never visibly "restarts".

Vertical + horizontal masters

Shoot or reframe 9:16 and 16:9 together, and serve each with <source media>.

Detail inserts

Two-second macros of coffee pouring, linen and keys. They work as hover previews on gallery cards.

Part five

The booking flow under the motion

Cinematics get the visitor to care, and the booking flow decides whether they pay you or Airbnb. Over 65% of rental-site traffic is on phones, so design this part on a phone first.

Dates above the fold

The date picker and guest count are visible without scrolling on every screen, sitting on top of the hero video and not hidden behind a button.

Sticky "Check dates"

On mobile, a bottom bar is always present. Pinned scroll scenes must never cover it. Tap targets are at least 44×44px.

Live availability

The calendar syncs in real time with the PMS, Airbnb and VRBO iCal. Stale calendars cause double bookings and refunds.

Total price up front

Show nightly rate × nights + cleaning + taxes before payment, and keep that sticky price line through checkout.

Guest checkout

Ask for dates, guests, name, email, phone and card, and nothing else. No account creation. Apple Pay and Google Pay go through Stripe.

Proof near the button

Put star rating, two short named guest quotes, and "Superhost on Airbnb" next to the CTA, not on a separate reviews page.

Instant confirmation

Show a confirmation page with check-in details, an add-to-calendar file and a map link, plus an email sent the same second.

Animate the booking too

Use @starting-style for the calendar popover, a Rive state machine for idle → loading → confirmed, and a view transition from the room to checkout.

Get found on Google

Add VacationRental JSON-LD: a stable ID, at least 8 images, lat/long to 5 decimals, and occupancy. Google Vacation Rentals then can link guests straight to the direct site.

Part six

Getting the footage: capture, generate, compress

One shoot day per property can supply every asset the site needs. This is the order to do it in.

  1. Gimbal and drone at golden hour. Shoot a 120fps walk-through for speed ramps, one drone push from the driveway (it becomes the scroll scrub), and day and dusk stills from a locked tripod (they become the clip-path reveal).

  2. Splat capture with a phone. Scaniverse is free, processes on-device in about a minute and exports .spz directly, which is about 90% smaller than PLY. Polycam uses iPhone LiDAR for blank walls. Walk a slow spiral and vary your height. Capture one room per splat, since there's no streaming level-of-detail yet.

  3. Load splats natively. Three.js r186 added a built-in GaussianSplat class with SPZLoader on WebGPURenderer. That's one loader call plus scene.add(). Spark and GaussianSplats3D remain options for older setups.

  4. Fill the gaps with AI, labelled. Use Higgsfield or Seedance image-to-video for neighbourhood b-roll, weather and seasons, and branded intros. Never present a generated clip as the actual unit. Label anything illustrative so the listing stays honest.

  5. Compress for the web. Make a 6–10s loop in AV1 with an H.264 fallback, a WebP poster under 100KB, and a WebP frame sequence for the scrub.

# hero loop: AV1 primary, H.264 fallback, no audio
ffmpeg -i hero.mov -an -t 8 -vf "scale=1920:-2,fps=30" -c:v libsvtav1 -crf 34 -preset 6 hero.av1.mp4
ffmpeg -i hero.mov -an -t 8 -vf "scale=1920:-2,fps=30" -c:v libx264 -crf 24 -preset slow -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart hero.h264.mp4

# poster = first frame as WebP
ffmpeg -i hero.mov -frames:v 1 -vf "scale=1920:-2" -c:v libwebp -quality 72 hero-poster.webp

# scroll-scrub frame sequence from the drone clip
ffmpeg -i drone.mp4 -vf "fps=24,scale=1600:-2" -c:v libwebp -quality 70 frames/%04d.webp

House rules

Ship it without hurting the booking rate

  1. The poster is the LCP. Use a WebP still under 100KB. Video loads after it and never blocks the first paint.

  2. Transform and opacity only. Never animate width, top or left, and keep INP under 200ms.

  3. Respect reduced motion. Use prefers-reduced-motion or gsap.matchMedia(), and show the still instead of the loop.

  4. Cursor effects are desktop only. Gate them on (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine) and don't ship that code to phones.

  5. One big moment per page. Pick the scrub, the splat or the WebGL gallery, and keep everything around it quiet.

  6. Book button always reachable. Pinned sections must never trap the CTA. Put a sticky "Check dates" bar on mobile.

  7. Licensed footage only. Use the owner's own shoot, Pexels or Pixabay (commercial-OK), or clips generated with Higgsfield. Never ripped YouTube or competitor listings.
