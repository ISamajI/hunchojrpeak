# LTG PEAK: "How websites actually work" reel

A 25-second vertical reel (1080x1920) for IG Reels, TikTok and Shorts. It teaches how a website works in three beats (HTML, CSS, JS), shows a site building itself live on screen, then pivots to LTG PEAK and a DM call to action. Made with [[code-driven-motion-design]], so every word and colour can be changed in the scene JSON.

## Why it should spread
- **It teaches something.** "How does this work?" content gets saved and shared, and saves push reach.
- **The before and after.** Watching an ugly wireframe turn into a premium site is the most satisfying shot, so it sits at 6 seconds, before people scroll.
- **One clear action.** "DM PEAK" gives you a keyword to track, and you can automate the reply with ManyChat or similar.
- **Post it natively** on all three platforms the same day, and pin it to your profile.

## Edit it
- Change the copy, colours or timing in `motion-studio/scenes/ltgpeak-how-websites-work.json`, then run `node render.cjs scenes/ltgpeak-how-websites-work.json`.
- The demo client ("Nova Cuts") is made up. Replace it with a real client site once you have permission.
- The reel has no audio yet. Add a trending sound in-app, or set `meta.audio` to a track (120 BPM matches the beat pulse).
