Mile Zero

No shortcuts. Just miles.

Sweatsuit capsule · 2 design directions × 3 colorways
Styled as flat-lay shots, built from the existing tee graphics

Couldn't pull the Instagram photo — Instagram blocks it for outside tools, so I worked from how Eric Emanuel actually shoots a set: hoodie and sweatpants styled together on a seamless backdrop, laid flat with real fold shadows and a fabric grain, one bold logo instead of a paragraph of copy.

Still no photo of an actual model — this session doesn't have a photo-realistic image generator. What's below is as close as an illustrated flat-lay gets: Chenille Arch (a big arched wordmark across the chest, like a varsity patch) and Drip Back (the graffiti "MILE ZERO" stack across the back), each in three colorways. I swapped one neutral for Brick — the color of the wall behind him in his own photos.

Send whichever hits land — happy to push the graphics further (bigger, smaller, different tagline) once you know which direction he's leaning.
