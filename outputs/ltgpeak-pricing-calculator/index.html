How this works: overhead per site = total monthly overhead ÷ sites/month. Break-even cost = overhead per site + (hours × your hourly rate). Break-even price = cost ÷ (1 − target margin), so that margin is what's left once a site sells at that price. The bar under each card shows how much of your current price covers cost (colored) versus profit (empty) — the tick mark is where your target-margin price falls. Monthly items (like ad management) use the same per-unit overhead share as a one-time site — each is "one job" for the month.
