What it does for the shop

Built to bring in work

More calls from phones

A lot of people find a mechanic on their phone, often when something just went wrong. Every number is one tap, and Call / Directions / Book stay at the bottom of the screen.

Requests after hours

Someone with a problem at 9 PM can send their car, phone and issue right away. You call them back in the morning.

The check-engine moment becomes a booking

A dashboard-light guide explains 8 common warning lights, how urgent each is, and what to do. One button books a diagnostic with the form filled in.

A reason to skip the dealer

A mileage planner shows what service is usually due, and makes clear drivers keep their factory warranty without going back to the dealership.

Fewer "are you open?" calls

The live open/closed badge and today's highlighted hours answer the question before the phone rings.

Trust at first glance

Jim and Bill, ASE certification, 130+ years of combined experience, NJ emissions certification and real reviews, all up front.

It also loads fast, works on every phone size, can be used with a keyboard, and gives Google your hours, address and phone in a format it reads cleanly. No one can honestly promise a top spot on Google, but the site is built the way Google expects.
