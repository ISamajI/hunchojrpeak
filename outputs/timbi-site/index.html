Where our story grew

More than a restaurant. Part of the city we love.

Right where downtown Trenton begins, Timbí brings Dominican and Latin flavor to South Broad Street: chimis, yaroas, mofongo, birria and our oven-roasted chicharrón. We believe Trenton's best days are still ahead, and we're proud to grow with it.
