# Post 1 - They Don't Know Yet

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Wed Nov 11. Reel, 20-25 seconds. Continues in [[Post 2 - The Walk-In]].

## The idea

Find a well-loved local business with no website on Google Maps. Build their homepage without asking. End before they find out.

The top reel in your niche this year (1.18M views) is exactly "Google Maps listing with no website, then a finished site." Yours beats it because it's a real shop in a real town and there's a Part 2.

## Pick the business

- 4.5 stars or higher, 100+ reviews, no website on the listing
- A place locals know: barbershop, taqueria, bakery, pizza shop
- Close enough to walk into tomorrow
- A Spanish-speaking owner is a plus. You're one of the few who can pitch in both languages

## Hooks to test as Trial Reels

1. "This [Trenton] barbershop has 312 five-star reviews and no website. So we built one. They don't know yet."
2. "We built a website for a business that never asked for one."
3. "4.9 stars. Zero website. We fixed it without telling them."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Screen recording: Google Maps listing, slow zoom on the rating, then on the empty spot where the website button would be | Hook line |
| 3-6s | You outside the shop, phone in hand, 1 second. Then laptop opening | "So me and my cousin built it." |
| 6-15s | Build timelapse at 20x: blank page, their name goes in, their photos, hours, tap to call. Cut on the beat | "Their photos. Their hours. Their menu." then "Took us one night." |
| 15-20s | Finished site scrolling on a phone in your hand, in front of the shopfront | "They have no idea." |
| 20-23s | You pulling the shop door open. Cut to black mid-step | "Part 2 tomorrow." |

## Edit notes

- Cut every 1.5 to 2 seconds until the timelapse
- The door cut is the whole post. Cut hard, no fade, no logo after it
- The last frame should match the first so it loops

## Caption

```
[Shop name] in [town] has [312] five-star reviews and no website.

So we built them one. Didn't ask. They don't know yet.

Part 2 tomorrow: we walk in and show them.

Know the owner? Send them this before we get there 👀

Want yours built before you pay a cent? DM us DEMO + your business name.

#[town] #smallbusiness #webdesign #[barbershop]
```

## Why it should travel

- People in that town know the shop, so they send it to each other and to the owner
- The open loop brings people back for Part 2, and to your profile to look for it

## Keep it clean

Be complimentary about the business. The story is "great shop, missing website", never "look how bad this is." Use only what's public on their listing.
