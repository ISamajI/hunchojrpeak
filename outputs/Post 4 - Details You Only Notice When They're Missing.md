# Post 4 - Details You Only Notice When They're Missing

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Sat Nov 14. Reel, about 20 seconds. English and Spanish versions.

## Finished file

`media/reels/post4-details.mp4` (1080x1920, silent). Add music in Instagram when you post. The site footage is a screenshot capture, so text inside the phone is slightly soft; swap in a real phone screen recording if you want it sharper.

## The idea

Three close-ups of small things you built that a cheap site wouldn't have. Customers judge quality by details, so show each one being used.

## Hooks to test

1. "3 details on our websites you only notice when they're missing."
2. "The difference between a $50 website and a real one is these 3 things."
3. Spanish: "3 detalles que separan una página barata de una profesional."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Extreme close-up of a phone screen, thumb hovering | Hook line |
| 2-8s | **Mikonos:** the hours badge showing "Open" or "Closed" in real time | "1. It knows if the kitchen is open right now." |

| 14-19s | **JM Group:** page loads, phone number and "Free estimate" are the first things visible | "3. Leak at 2am? The number comes first." |
| 19-21s | Three sites side by side | "We think about your customer. DM DEMO." |

## Edit notes

- Zoom the screen recording to 150-200% on each detail so it fills the frame
- A soft click or ding as each detail lands
- Slow, steady pacing. This one should feel calm and precise
