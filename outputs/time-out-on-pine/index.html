    Is it 21+?

    Yes. Valid ID is required for nightlife events. Wednesday trivia is aimed at adults.

    Is there a cover?

    It varies by event. Trivia, Thursday Social, Run It Back and Karaoke are usually free. Nashe Party is $10 at the door.

    Do I need to RSVP?

    It’s strongly recommended for high-demand nights. For trivia, RSVP if you’re bringing 6 or more.

    Is there a dress code?

    No strict one, though some nights feel more like a real night out. There’s no smoking or vaping indoors.

    How do deposits work for private events?

    A 25% deposit, based on the estimated event minimum, secures the booking. Final guest count and food and drink picks are due five days before.
