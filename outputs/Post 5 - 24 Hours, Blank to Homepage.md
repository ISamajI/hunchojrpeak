# Post 5 - 24 Hours, Blank to Homepage

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Sun Nov 15. Reel, 15-20 seconds. Pin it.

## Finished file

`media/reels/post5-24-hours.mp4` (1080x1920, silent). Add music in Instagram when you post. The site footage is a screenshot capture, so text inside the phone is slightly soft; swap in a real phone screen recording if you want it sharper.

This video is an animated recreation of the SariNails homepage coming together, labelled on screen as sarinails.us. It is not a recording of a real 24-hour build. For a stronger version, screen-record your next real demo and use the same text and clock.

## The idea

Your site promises a real homepage in 24 hours. Show one being built with a clock running. Screen and hands only.

## What to build

A real demo for a real request if you have one this week. If not, build a sample for a made-up business ("Luna's Bakery, South Philly") and say so in the caption. Record the whole screen while you work.

## Hooks to test

1. "You asked for a website at 9am. This is what you get by 9am tomorrow."
2. "Blank page to finished homepage in 24 hours. Watch."
3. "We build your website before you pay. Here's one being built."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Blank white screen, cursor blinking. Clock in the corner at 00:00 | Hook line |
| 2-5s | Hands on keyboard, 1 second. Then the business name appears on the page | "Hour 1: your name, your colors" |
| 5-10s | Timelapse at 30x: photos drop in, services, hours, map. Clock spinning | "Hour 6: your photos, your services" |
| 10-14s | Tap-to-call button added, then the page shrinks to phone size | "Hour 20: tested on a phone" |
| 14-18s | Finished homepage scrolling on a phone. Clock stops at 24:00 | "Hour 24: in your hands. $0 so far." |
| 18-20s | Text only | "Love it, we finish in days. Don't, you owe nothing. DM DEMO" |
