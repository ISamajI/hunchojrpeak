Call script

Open: "Hey, is this the owner? My name's Henry, I run LTGPEAK, a web design company out of Mercer County. I came across your shop and noticed you don't have your own website yet. Got 30 seconds?"

En español: "¿Hola, hablo con el dueño? Me llamo Henry, tengo una compañía de diseño web, LTGPEAK. Vi que su negocio no tiene página web propia todavía. ¿Tiene 30 segundos?"

  1. Point to the gap. "People search you on Google and land on Yelp or Facebook. That's where they compare you to the next shop."
  2. The offer. "We build it in one to two days, with booking or a menu so customers can act right away."
  3. The ask. "Can I send you a free mock-up of what yours would look like? What's the best number or email?"

"Not interested": "No problem. Mind if I text you the mock-up anyway? No charge."
"How much?": "Depends what you need. I'll send the mock-up with a price so you see both together."
