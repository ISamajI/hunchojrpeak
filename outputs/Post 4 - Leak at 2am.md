# Post 4 - Leak at 2am

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Sat Nov 14. Reel, about 15 seconds. English and Spanish versions.

## The idea

Your JM Group Contractors site was "built for a leak at 2am: the free estimate and the phone number come first." That line is already a scene. Film it.

## Hooks to test as Trial Reels

1. "POV: it's 2am and your ceiling is dripping."
2. "Your roof is leaking. You have 10 seconds to pick a roofer."
3. Spanish: "Son las 2am y te está goteando el techo."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Dark room. Water dripping into a pot. Phone screen lights up a face | "2:07 AM" then hook line |
| 2-6s | Thumb types "roofer near me". Opens a cluttered site: slideshow, tiny text, pop-up, no number in sight. Frustrated scroll | "Where's the phone number??" |
| 6-10s | Back. Opens jmgroupcontractors.com. Number and "Free estimate" are right there. Thumb taps call | "Number first. One tap." |
| 10-13s | Phone to ear. Drip sound stops on the cut | "That's who gets the job." |
| 13-15s | Site on phone, clean | "We built this one. DM DEMO for yours." |

## Edit notes

- For the "bad" site, mock one up yourself. Don't show a real competitor's site
- Sound carries this reel: drip, typing clicks, ring tone. Keep music very low or off
- Shoot it actually dark, lit only by the phone
