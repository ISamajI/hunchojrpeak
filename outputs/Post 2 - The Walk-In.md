# Post 2 - The Walk-In

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Thu Nov 12. Reel, 30-45 seconds. Pays off [[Post 1 - They Don't Know Yet]].

## The idea

Walk in, hand the owner a phone with their new website on it, and film what happens. Raw beats polished here.

## Before you film

- Ask "mind if I film this?" as you start. If they say no to being on camera, film only the phone and your hands and keep their voice out
- Go at a slow hour. Not the lunch rush
- One cousin films, one talks. Hold the phone vertical at chest height
- Have the site open and loaded. No waiting on Wi-Fi

## Hooks to test as Trial Reels

1. "We built this barbershop a website without asking. Here's the owner seeing it."
2. "Walking into a business to show them a website they never ordered."
3. "He thought I was selling something. Then he saw his own shop on my phone."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | 2-second recap of yesterday's finished site, then the door opening | Hook line |
| 3-10s | "Hey, are you the owner? We built you something." Hand over the phone | Captions of what's said |
| 10-25s | Hold on their face and hands as they scroll. Don't cut. Let silence sit | "That's his shop. His prices. His hours." |
| 25-35s | Their first real reaction or question. Your answer: "It's free to look at. You only pay if you want it." | "$0 to see it. That's the whole deal." |
| 35-40s | The handshake, the "let me think", or the "no thanks". Whatever really happened | "DM us DEMO. Yours is next." |

## If they say no

Post it anyway, with their OK. A polite "no" is honest and people watch it to the end. Change the last text to "He said no. We're doing another one next week." Rejection content often outperforms wins, and it sets up the series.

## Caption

```
Yesterday we built [shop name] a website without asking.

Today we walked in and showed [owner's first name].

[One honest line: what he said.]

This is how we work with everybody. You see the real site first. Love it, we finish it in days. Don't, you owe nothing.

Which [town] business should we do next? Send this to the owner.

DM us DEMO + your business name and yours is ready in 24 hours.

#[town] #smallbusiness #webdesign
```

## After posting

- Ask the shop to share it to their Story and invite them as a Collab if they're on Instagram
- Pin a comment: "Who's next? Drop a [town] business with no website 👇"
- Every business named in the comments is a lead and a possible next episode
