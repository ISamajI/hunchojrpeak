# Post 3 - We Checked 50 Businesses

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Fri Nov 13. Carousel, 7 slides.

## The idea

Spend one hour on Google Maps counting. Pick one category in one town, for example 50 restaurants in Trenton or 50 barbershops in North Philly. Record what you find. Your own local numbers are something no other account has, and owners send it to each other asking "are we one of these?"

Carousels are also what people save, and a save brings them back when they're ready to buy.

## What to count (takes about an hour)

For each of the 50, open it on your phone and tally:

- No website at all
- Website link is just Facebook or Instagram
- Menu or price list is a photo you have to pinch to read
- Hours on the site don't match Google
- No tap-to-call button
- Takes longer than 5 seconds to load on phone data

Use the real counts. Don't round up, and don't name or show the businesses that failed.

7. **CTA.** "Is yours one of the 50? DM us DEMO + your business name. We'll build your homepage free in 24 hours. Like it or owe nothing."
