    About J&B

    Small enough to know you. Big enough to fix anything.

    J&B Automotive is a family-owned shop that has served Mercerville, Hamilton, and all of Mercer County since 1978. Co-owners Jim and Bill still put their hands on the work — from modern engines to classic cars.
