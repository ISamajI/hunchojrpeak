# Post 7 - Small Business Saturday Build

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Tue Nov 17. Reel, 15-20 seconds, plus a Story countdown.

## The idea

Small Business Saturday is Nov 28. Eleven days out, announce that the community picks one local business and you build its full website free, live by that Saturday. It gives everyone who watched the week a reason to act now, and gives you eleven more days of content: nominations, the pick, the build, the launch.

**Your call before posting:** this gives away an $800 Standard site. If that's too much, the same post works with "free homepage demo for the first 10 businesses nominated", which you already do for free.

## Hooks to test as Trial Reels

1. "We're building one [Philly/NJ] business a full website for free. You pick who."
2. "Small Business Saturday is in 11 days. One local business is getting a free website."
3. "Tell us the best local business with the worst website. We'll fix it for free."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Both of you to camera, on a busy local street | Hook line |
| 3-8s | Fast cuts from the week: the walk-in, the 2am phone, the booking race | "This week we built for a barber, a roofer, a nail studio." |
| 8-14s | Back to you. Explain the rules in one breath | "1. Send this to the owner. 2. Comment their business. Most nominated wins." |
| 14-18s | Calendar graphic: Nov 28 circled | "Live by Small Business Saturday." |

## Caption

```
Small Business Saturday is November 28.

We're building ONE local business a full website, free. Live by that Saturday. You pick who.

How:
1. Send this to the owner so they know
2. Comment the business name and town
3. We announce the pick Friday Nov 20

Philly, Trenton, Ewing, Camden County, South Jersey. Restaurants, shops, barbers, trades. Hablamos español.

Not picked? Everyone nominated still gets a free homepage demo. DM us DEMO.

This giveaway is not sponsored by or affiliated with Instagram.

#smallbusinesssaturday #shoplocal #philly #newjersey #smallbusiness
```

## Run it for the eleven days

- **Story countdown sticker** to Nov 28, today
- **Daily Story:** screenshot the best nomination, tag the business
- **Fri Nov 20:** announce the pick, in person, on camera. That's another walk-in reel
- **Nov 21-27:** build diary in Stories
- **Sat Nov 28:** launch reel, Collab with the winner

Every nominated business that doesn't win is a warm lead. Send each one a free demo that week.
