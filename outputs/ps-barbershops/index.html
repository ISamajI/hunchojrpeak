FAQ
Good to know.
Still have a question? Text or call (951) 475-0932.

      

        
Do you take walk-ins?+
Yes. Both shops welcome walk-ins every day from 9am to 9pm. To hold a chair, text us your shop, service and time.

        
How much is a haircut?+
Every haircut is $35, including fades, tapers and kids cuts. Designs, line-ups and beard work are $10 each on top.

        
Are prices the same at both locations?+
Yes. The Edge and Sharp & Faded have the same prices and the same hours.

        
Do you cut kids' hair?+
Yes. Kids cuts for ages 3 to 12 are $35, and we cut every age after that too.

        
How do I book?+
Use the booking form above. It writes your text for you, then you send it to (951) 475-0932.
