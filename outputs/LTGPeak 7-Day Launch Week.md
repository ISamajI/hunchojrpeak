## The one idea behind all seven

Nobody appears on camera. Every post shows evidence a customer can check for themselves: live sites, real domains, test scores, the fine print. Other agencies say "quality" in a caption. You show it on screen.

## How to shoot faceless

- **Screen recordings** of the live sites on a phone. This is most of the footage.
- **Hands only:** a phone held in front of the client's shopfront, or hands on a keyboard.
- **Text on screen carries the message.** Add a voiceover if one of you is comfortable; your voice isn't your face. Otherwise text and music only.
- **One look for everything:** the same two colors and one font from ltgpeak.app on every post, so the grid itself looks like quality work.

## Rules for every post

1. **Hook in text in the first second.** Never open on a logo.
2. **Show, then claim.** The proof is on screen before the caption says anything.
3. **Name the place:** Philly, Ewing, Pennsauken, South Jersey.
4. **One CTA:** "DM us DEMO and your business name."
5. **End each caption by saying who to send it to.** Sends are what get a reel shown to non-followers.
6. **Invite the client as a Collab** whenever their site is featured.
7. **Export clean:** 1080x1920 for reels, 1080x1350 for carousels, no app watermark.

## Test hooks with Trial Reels

Trial Reels go only to non-followers and stay off your grid. Post two or three versions of each reel with a different first line, wait 24 hours, then put the winner on the main feed.

## Fix first (20 minutes)

- **Bio:** `See your website before you pay. Free demo in 24 hrs. Philly · NJ · EN/ES. DM "DEMO" 👇`
- **Link:** ltgpeak.app/#book
- **Highlights:** Work, How it works, Prices, Español
- **Your own site contradicts itself.** The top of ltgpeak.app says "5 change rounds included." The fine print says "2 rounds included. A third round is $100." Fix that before this week. A customer checking your quality will read both.

## What a good week looks like

Nobody can promise a viral post. Proof content usually spreads slower than stunts, but the people it reaches are closer to buying. A good week is steady 3,000 to 10,000 views per reel, a profile that convinces whoever lands on it, and 5 to 10 demo requests.

## Numbers you can safely quote

- 72% of consumers look at three or fewer businesses before deciding (BrightLocal, 2026).
- 78% of consumers say they have abandoned a business after an unanswered call (CallRail survey of 1,000 US consumers, 2025).

Skip "85% of callers never call back" and "97% of people Google a business first." Neither traces back to real research.
