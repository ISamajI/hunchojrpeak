# LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week

Seven posts for @ltgpeak, Wed Nov 11 to Tue Nov 17, 2026. Built from [[LTGPeak Account Audit]] and [[Instagram Reach Signals 2026]].

## The one idea behind all seven

Your offer is the content. "We build your website before you pay" is a story people can watch happen. Every agency in your niche posts "your business deserves a website" and gets 4 to 450 views. The reels that travel show a real business, a real build, and a real reaction.

So the week is one running story: we find a local business with no website, build it without asking, walk in, and show them. Everything else supports that story.

## Rules for every post

1. **Hook in the first second, on screen, in text.** If the first frame is your logo, the viewer is gone.
2. **Design for the send.** Every caption ends by telling the viewer who to send it to. Sends per reach is the signal that gets a reel shown to people who don't follow you.
3. **Name the place.** Say "Philly", "Trenton", "Ewing", "Pennsauken", "South Jersey" in the on-screen text, the voiceover, and the caption. Local reels get sent to local people.
4. **One CTA, always the same:** "DM us DEMO and your business name." It matches what ltgpeak.app already says.
5. **Real voice over the video.** Record a voiceover, then lay a trending track under it at 5-10% volume. Pick the track from Instagram's own trending list on the day you post.
6. **Burn in captions.** Most people watch muted.
7. **Export clean.** 1080x1920, no TikTok or CapCut watermark.
8. **Use Collab posts.** Invite the client's account as a collaborator on Posts 4 and 6. The reel then shows on their profile and reaches their followers too. Your SariNails reel with the tag got 13 comments; the generic ones got 0 to 4.
9. **Answer every comment in the first hour**, with a question back.

## Test hooks with Trial Reels

Trial Reels go only to non-followers and don't show on your grid. Before each main reel, post two or three trial versions with a different first line and the same body. Give them 24 hours. Put the winner on the main feed as a fresh upload with a small change, since Instagram suppresses near-duplicates.

With 31 followers this is nearly free: you have no audience to annoy, and each trial is another shot at strangers.

## The work that isn't posting

- **Send the demo yourself.** Each day, DM five local businesses a 10-second screen recording of a homepage you built for them. This is the offer on your site, done outbound. It also produces footage for the next reels.
- **Cross-post** the same clean files to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels. Facebook Reels reach local owners over 40 better than Instagram does.
- **Ask Mikonos, SariNails and JM Group** to share the reel they're in to their Stories.

## What "blowing up" can honestly mean in a week

Nobody can promise a viral post. You're starting from 31 followers after 40 days of no posts. But your reels already reach 2,000 to 4,000 people each without followers, so reach is not the problem. A good week looks like one reel past 20,000 to 50,000 views in your area, a few hundred followers who are local owners, and 5 to 15 demo requests. For a business selling $500 to $1,500 sites, three of those closing pays for the whole week many times over.

If Post 1 and 2 work, don't stop. Make it a weekly series with a new business every week.

## Numbers you can safely quote

- 72% of consumers look at three or fewer businesses before deciding (BrightLocal, 2026).

Skip "85% of callers never call back" and "97% of people Google a business first." They're everywhere, and neither traces back to real research.
