# Post 6 - The Booking Race

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Mon Nov 16. Reel, 15-20 seconds, split screen with a timer.

## The idea

A race. Left side: booking a nail appointment by DM. Right side: booking on sarinails.us. A running clock on both. People watch races to the end to see the gap, and watch time is one of the three signals Instagram ranks on.

Your SariNails post is already your most commented one. This gives that client a second, stronger outing.

## Hooks to test as Trial Reels

1. "Booking a nail appointment by DM vs. on a real booking site. Timed."
2. "Nail techs: this is how many clients you lose in your DMs."
3. "She stopped answering 'what times you got?' forever. Here's how."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Split screen appears, both timers at 0:00 | Hook line. Labels: "DM" and "Website" |
| 2-12s | **Left:** types "hey do you have anything Saturday?" ... waiting ... "seen" ... still waiting. Clock jumps: 4 min, 1 hr, 3 hrs. **Right:** picks service, picks time, pays deposit, confirmation. Clock stops at about 0:20 | Right side freezes with a green check: "Booked. Deposit paid. 0:20" |
| 12-16s | Left side still waiting. Clock reads "3 hrs 12 min" | "Still waiting. She's with a client." |
| 16-20s | Full screen: reminder text arriving on the phone | "And it texts the reminder itself. DM DEMO." |
