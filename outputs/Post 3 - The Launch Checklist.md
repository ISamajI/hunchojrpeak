# Post 3 - The Launch Checklist

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Fri Nov 13. Carousel, 8 slides.

## The idea

Show the checks every site passes before it goes live. A written standard tells a customer that quality is built into how you work. Each slide pairs the check with a screenshot from a real client site proving it.

## Slides

1. **Cover:** "No site leaves us until it passes these 7 checks." Small: "Swipe. Save it. Hold your designer to it."
2. **Built on a phone first.** "Most of your customers are on their phone." Screenshot: a client site on mobile.
3. **Tap to call.** "One tap, the phone rings. No copying numbers." Screenshot: JM Group call button.
4. **Hours and map that are right.** "Wrong hours send people to the next place." Screenshot: Mikonos live hours.
5. **The lock in the address bar.** "HTTPS on every site. No 'Not secure' warning." Screenshot: address bar with the lock.
6. **A form that reaches you.** "We send a test message before launch. It lands in your inbox." Screenshot: contact form.
7. **Your domain, in your name.** "You own the address and the content. Leave any time and take them with you."
8. **CTA:** "Want to see yours pass all 7? DM DEMO + your business name. Free homepage in 24 hours."

## Design

- 1080x1350. Check name huge at the top, one line under it, screenshot in a phone frame below
- A green check mark in the same corner on slides 2 to 7
- Slide count in the corner ("3/8") keeps people swiping
