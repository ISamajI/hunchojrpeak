# Post 3 - The Launch Checklist

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Fri Nov 13. Carousel, 9 slides.

## The idea

Show the checks every site passes before it goes live. A written standard tells a customer that quality is built into how you work. Each slide pairs the check with a screenshot from a real client site proving it.

## Slides

Finished files: `media/post3-checklist/slide-01.png` to `slide-09.png`. Upload in order.

1. **Cover:** "No site leaves us until it passes 7 checks."
2. **Built on a phone first.** SariNails on a phone.
3. **Tap to call.** Mikonos, call button ringed.
4. **Hours that are right, right now.** Mikonos "Open now" badge ringed.
5. **The lock in the address bar.** JM Group with the secure address bar.
6. **A form that reaches you.** Test message before launch.
7. **Loads fast on phone data.** Points to pagespeed.web.dev.
8. **Your domain, in your name.** The three live domains.
9. **CTA:** "Want to see yours pass all 7? DM us DEMO + your business name."

## Design

- 1080x1350. Check name huge at the top, one line under it, screenshot in a phone frame below
- A green check mark in the same corner on slides 2 to 7
- Slide count in the corner ("03 / 09") keeps people swiping
