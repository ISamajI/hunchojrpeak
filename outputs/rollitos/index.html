The show, in four moves

How a roll is born

It takes about two minutes from pour to cup. Grab a seat by the counter, because this part is the whole point.

STEP 1

Pour

A fresh sweet cream base hits a plate held well below freezing.

STEP 2

Chop

Fruit, cookies or cheesecake get chopped straight into the cream with two spatulas.

STEP 3

Spread

The mix is pressed into a paper-thin sheet that sets in seconds.

STEP 4

Roll

Scraped into curls, stood up in the cup, then piled with toppings and drizzle.

Our story
"We started with a cart and one goal: bring joy through flavor."

Ginger and Eduardo Salazar spent their weekends rolling ice cream at parties, festivals and family events across the area. The lines kept getting longer, so they gave the cart a permanent home on Whitehorse-Mercerville Road.
