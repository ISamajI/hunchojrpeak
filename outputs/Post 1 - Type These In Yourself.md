## The idea

Most web designers show mockups. You show three live sites on the owners' own domains and dare the viewer to check. It's the fastest way to prove the work exists.

## Hooks to test

1. "Don't trust a web designer's mockups. Type these in yourself."
2. "3 websites we built. All live right now. Go check."
3. "Anyone can post a mockup. Here are the real addresses."

## Edit notes

- Record on a real phone, not a desktop mock. Keep the address bar in frame the whole time; it's the proof
- Cut on the beat at each site change
- Hold each address on screen long enough to read
