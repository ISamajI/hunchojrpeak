# Post 6 - Template vs Built for You

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Mon Nov 16. Reel, about 15 seconds, split screen.

## The idea

Side by side: a generic template on the left, a site you built on the right, same type of business. The viewer sees the quality gap without being told.

## Build the left side yourself

Make the "template" side from scratch: stock photo, "Welcome to our website", "Lorem ipsum", a "Learn more" button that goes nowhere. Don't use a real business's site or a named builder's theme.

## Hooks to test

1. "Left: a $50 template. Right: built for the business. Same price range. Look closer."
2. "How to tell if your web designer just dropped your logo on a template."
3. "Both of these are 'a website.' Only one gets you customers."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Split screen, two phones, both loading | Hook line. Labels: "Template" and "Built for you" |
| 2-5s | Both scroll the top. Left: stock photo of strangers. Right: the client's real work | "Stock photo vs. their real work" |
| 5-8s | Left: "Welcome to our website." Right: what they do and where | "Filler words vs. what customers ask" |

| 13-15s | Text only | "See yours first. Free. DM DEMO" |

## Edit notes

- Both sides scroll at the same speed so the comparison is fair
- Red X on the left and green check on the right as each point lands
- The right side should be the client site that best matches the template's business type
