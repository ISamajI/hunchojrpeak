# Post 5 - Two Cousins

Part of [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]]. Sun Nov 15. Reel, about 20 seconds, face to camera. Pin it.

## The idea

By Sunday, strangers from Posts 1 to 4 are landing on your profile asking "who are these guys?" Right now the page has no faces on it. People hire people, and "two cousins" is easier to trust and remember than "a creative digital team."

## Hooks to test as Trial Reels

1. "Me and my cousin build websites for free. You only pay if you like it."
2. "We're two cousins from [Philly]. We'll build your website before you pay us a dollar."
3. "Everyone told us giving away the work first was a dumb business model."

## Script

Both of you in frame, outside, daylight, walking or leaning on a car. One take feel. Trade lines.

> **A:** Me and my cousin build websites for local businesses.
> **B:** And we build it before you pay.
> **A:** You tell us what you do. Twenty minutes.
> **B:** Next day you get a real homepage. Your name, your photos, your services.
> **A:** Love it? We finish it in three days. From five hundred dollars.
> **B:** Don't love it? You owe us nothing. For real.
> **A:** We've done a nail studio in Philly, a Greek spot in Ewing, a roofer in Pennsauken.
> **B:** You're next. DM us "demo."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Both in frame, first line said straight to camera | Hook line |
| 3-12s | Jump cut between you on each line. On "real homepage", flash 1 second of a site on a phone | Captions, key words in your accent color |
| 12-17s | Three half-second flashes: SariNails, Mikonos, JM Group sites | "Philly · Ewing · Pennsauken" |
| 17-20s | Both back in frame | "DM DEMO. EN / ES." |

## Edit notes

- Use a clip-on or wired mic. Bad audio loses people faster than bad video
- No intro, no "hey guys". The first word is the hook
- Cut every breath between lines
