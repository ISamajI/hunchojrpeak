About us

100% dedicated to the success of your project.

For over 40 years we've worked closely with landscape owners, contractors and developers across New Jersey, New York, Delaware and Eastern Pennsylvania to help them realize their visions.

Our story

A family-run company that still shows up on site.

Sean's Landscaping is a full-service commercial landscaping company based in Hamilton, New Jersey.

Our leadership has grown up inside the business. President Gerry Brady joined in 1985 and worked his way up through every role; today he leads the team and is on site with the crews on every job. Our senior project managers have each been with us for more than two decades.

That continuity is why general contractors, hospitals and universities keep calling us back — RWJ Hospital Hamilton has been a client since 1994.
