# Verified renders

- One paused, deterministic timeline per scene; animate transforms only.
- Select exact frames in one pass (separate encodes + concat drift A/V sync).
- Anchor beats to exact transcript phrases and validate them; see [[beat-synced-captions]].
- Per render: preflight, contact sheet of hero and transition frames, `VERIFY.md` with evidence.
- Sites: serve over HTTP, real Chrome, screenshots at desktop, 390×844 and reduced motion.
- "Never claim a lint result proves visual quality."
