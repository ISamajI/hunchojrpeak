# Instagram Reach Signals 2026

How Instagram decides who sees a reel, as of Nov 2026. Used in [[LTGPeak 7-Day Launch Week]].

## The three signals

Instagram head Adam Mosseri names three: **watch time, likes, and sends** (DM shares). He tracks them as average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach.

- **Sends per reach** matters most for reaching people who don't follow you. In October 2026 he said Instagram weighs sends "a lot more" than comments.
- **Watch time** matters everywhere, most of all on Reels. The first 3 seconds decide whether someone stays.
- **Likes per reach** matters more for reaching your existing followers.

Follower count has little to do with it. Reels are almost entirely recommended content.

## How to read it in Insights

- Sends per reach = Shares ÷ Accounts reached
- Hook strength = share of viewers who stay past 3 seconds. Above about 60% is a working hook
- Strong 3-second rate but short average watch means the hook works and the middle drags

## Trial Reels

- A toggle before posting. The reel goes only to non-followers and stays off your grid
- Use it to test hooks: same body, different first line, one change per test
- Give each 24 to 72 hours, spaced a day apart
- Don't re-upload a reel that's already on your feed. Instagram detects near-duplicates and limits them
- Keep reels under 3 minutes. Longer ones aren't recommended to new audiences
- Trials can be scheduled in advance (added April 2026)
