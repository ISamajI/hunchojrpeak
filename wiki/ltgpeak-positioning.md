# LTGPEAK positioning

Business-side teachers (Nate, Nick Saraev, Liam Ottley, Jono Catliff) teach selling; engineers (Cole Medin, IndyDevDan) teach reliability. Nobody does both for people selling to small businesses.

Angle: agents that pass review, results shown with real numbers. Ranked bets:
1. Security and QA teardowns of public templates, run by [[Itachi]]
2. Measured multi-agent design (1 vs 2 vs 3 agents on one real task)
3. A code-driven ad factory ("10 ads in 48 hours" pilots)
4. Public build logs with real local clients
5. A free starter kit instead of another paid Skool

Part of [[nate-herk-playbook]].
