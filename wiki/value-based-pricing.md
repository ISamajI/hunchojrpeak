# Value-based pricing

- Discovery: "If you had 10x the business tomorrow, what would break first?" Then silence. Listen, Repeat, Poke.
- Price at 10–20% of first-year value (appointment setter worth $41,600/yr → $5,500).
- Proposal: 3 paragraphs about the client, 3 tiers, middle built to win. Milestones ~30 days apart, each objective.
- Budget pushback: cut scope, never price. Flat maintenance retainer (he used $400/mo).
- Pin one KPI before payment, report at months 1/2/3 on a dashboard, then pitch the retainer.
