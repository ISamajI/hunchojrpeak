# Beat-synced captions

- frame = seconds × fps
- one beat = fps × 60 / bpm frames (120 BPM at 30 fps is 15 frames)
- Put cuts and caption changes on beats. The hook goes in the first 30 frames.
- Keep each caption to 1–4 words with one highlighted word, on screen for at least 1s.

Part of [[code-driven-motion-design]].
