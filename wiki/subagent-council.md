# Subagent council

- Advisors get `tools: Read, Grep, Glob` and `maxTurns: 12`; only executors write.
- Each has a short always-read brief (`_hot.md`, 600–800 words) with a "where this lens misfires" section.
- The chair never argues. It seats 5–7 (for us 3–7), always a downside guard and the likeliest dissenter.
- Open → debate → vote → scored verdict saved to `sessions/<date>-<slug>/`.

Upgrade path for the [[Jarvis system]] and [[Akamaru system]]: branch lead = chair, [[Itachi]] = downside guard. Part of [[nate-herk-playbook]].
