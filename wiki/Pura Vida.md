# Pura Vida

Demo revamp pitch for Pura Vida (Costa Rican food, 228 Cummings Ave, Trenton NJ). One hub page with five full versions, all using the real menu, prices, hours and online-ordering link.

## Versions
- **V1 La Carreta** — spinning oxcart-wheel menu
- **V2 Reloj Tico** — sky follows Trenton time; drag the sun to see what to order
- **V3 Dulce Noche** — dark dessert lounge
- **V4 Vuelo 228** — split-flap departures board + boarding-pass ordering
- **V5 Selva** — rainforest scroll
