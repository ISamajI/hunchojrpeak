# Cold Call Outreach Workflow

How [[LTGPEAK]] finds and closes local clients.

## 1 · Build the lead list
- Target: Mercer County (Trenton/Hamilton/Ewing area), **4★ and up on Google**, with **no website, a social-only link, or a site that needs work**.
- Group by type: barbers & salons, food, auto & trades, shops.
- Each lead card gets a "Say this" line pulled from the business's own Google reviews.
- Tracked in the **LTGPEAK Call Sheet** artifact (Leads → Called → Call back → Interested → Demo booked).

## 2 · The 60-second call
1. **Open** — "Hey, is this the owner? I'm [name] with LTGPEAK. We build websites for local spots around Trenton."
2. **Hook** — read their "Say this" line from their reviews.
3. **Offer** — free demo site in 24 hours; if they don't love it, they owe nothing.
4. **Close** — best number or Instagram to send it to; ask them to text photos + logo.
- Spanish leads: "Le hacemos un demo gratis de su página web en 24 horas, en inglés y español. Si no le gusta, no paga nada."

## 3 · Demo → proposal
- Build the demo with the [[Client Website Build Workflow]].
- Send a proposal page (e.g. [[J&B Automotive]]: $650 one-time + $60/mo care plan).
