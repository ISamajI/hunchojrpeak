## What this says

- **Reach isn't the problem.** Reels pull 2,000 to 4,000 plays with 31 followers, so Instagram is already showing them to strangers.
- **Nothing makes a stranger act.** Like rates sit around 1%, and no post asks for a DM.
- **Client posts get talked about.** The SariNails reel, with the client tagged, has the most comments. The generic promo got zero.
- **No proof, no place.** Nothing shows the work being used, or says where you work.

## What the website says that Instagram doesn't

ltgpeak.app leads with a much stronger pitch than the Instagram page: "See your new website before you pay." Free demo in 24 hours, $0 to look, sites from $500, English and Spanish, three live clients in Philadelphia, Ewing and Pennsauken. None of that is on Instagram yet.

## What works for others in this niche

From a sample of about 30 reels on local business websites, past 12 months:

| Views | Format |
|---|---|
| 1,180,000 | Google Maps listing with no website, then a site built for it |
| 954 | Walked up to a local business and built the site on the spot |
| 641 | Before and after of redesigned sites |
| 4 to 450 | Agency promos: "Is your website losing you customers?", "Still don't have a website?" |

The promo style that @ltgpeak has been posting is the weakest format in the sample. Real business plus real process plus a result is the strongest.

Caveat: this is one search sample, not a full study.
