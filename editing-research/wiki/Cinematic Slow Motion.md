# Cinematic Slow Motion

Exaggerated slow-motion entrances or emotional moments set to dramatic audio, applying film language to everyday or sentimental footage.

## How to do it
1. Shoot at 60fps or higher where possible, on a wide, steady framing.
2. Slow the clip in [[CapCut]] or TikTok's speed tool, or use a [[Speed Ramp]].
3. Time each entrance to the music.
- Wedding edits: 5–8 clips, soft moments first, the biggest moment on the song's final line, simple transitions.

## Why it works
Movie-trailer language on an ordinary setting creates comic contrast, or it lifts sentimental footage.

## Examples (2026)
"Pray For Me Slow Mo" (Nov 2026, friends enter the frame like an action-movie opening); "Patient Zero Wedding Day" cinematic wedding edits (Nov 2026); "I will do anything for you" (Jul 2026, overlay text, suspense audio, slow-mo).
