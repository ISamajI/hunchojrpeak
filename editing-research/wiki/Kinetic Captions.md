# Kinetic Captions

Burned-in captions that appear word by word (1–4 words at a time) in sync with speech or the beat, with the active or key word highlighted and a pop or bounce animation. Named styles: "Hormozi style" (bold sans-serif, often all caps, yellow/green highlight), "pastel pop", and "minimal clean" (white, subtle opacity).

## How to do it
- **Auto-caption, then style**: [[CapCut]] Auto Captions / AI Captions; TikTok native auto-captions; [[Edits]] auto-highlighting captions with per-word styling and saved caption styles (Aug 2026); [[Premiere Mobile]] animated captions; [[Adobe Premiere Pro]] 26.3 single-word captions (Text panel > Transcribe > Create captions; per retailer blog EditorsKeys, not Adobe release notes); [[Captions]] (75+ styles incl. "Negative Captions"); [[Submagic]]; [[Opus Clip]]; [[Descript]]; [[Final Cut Pro]] (23 new transcription languages).
- **X composer** (Oct 2026 refresh): generate captions, fix them or ask Grok to correct them, optionally translate. For styled kinetic captions, burn them in with an external editor first.
- **Style tips**: free fonts Anton or Montserrat Black/ExtraBold (SIL OFL); restyle key words in a contrasting color; centered, lower-middle placement, kept out of the bottom UI zone ([[Safe Zones]]).
- **LinkedIn spec (vendor guidance)**: bold sans-serif, white/yellow with dark stroke, ≤42 characters per line, 1–2 lines, sentence case, lower third above the buttons.

## Why it works
Most feeds autoplay muted, so captions carry the video for sound-off viewers and guide the eye. Evidence on lift is weak: Exemplary AI found **no controlled study** showing Hormozi captions increase views. OpusClip calls captions "the most important retention mechanism" in brainrot edits (vendor). A LinkedIn claim of 40–60% higher watch time from well-formatted captions is vendor-sourced.

## Status
Peak / saturated overall, but the raw notes disagree on direction:
- TikTok and Instagram notes: peak / staple. Most of the evidence is caption-tool vendor blogs.
- YouTube note: peak on Shorts but **fading as a signature look**, because it is everywhere and preset-driven. This is the research agent's inference and is not stated by any source.
- Other Platforms note: **rising** on X, where captions are now the main step in the native composer.
- The "minimal clean" style fits the [[Lo-Fi Aesthetic]] shift.
