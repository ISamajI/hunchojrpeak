# Snapchat Spotlight

Platform hub. Snap is the most explicit platform in 2026 about rewarding camera-native, human-made content and demoting fully AI-generated and syndicated posts.

## Techniques
- **Rising**: [[Lo-Fi Aesthetic]] (camera-native, authentic content)
- **Peak**: [[Beat Sync]] via Quick Cut (launched 2025-12-17) · [[Template Remix]] (Quick Cut remix) · AR Lens effects as the edit layer (official Snap creator guidance; covered in [[Lo-Fi Aesthetic]])
- **Penalized**: fully AI-generated [[AI Video Generation]]; recycled, watermarked TikTok/Reels uploads

## Key 2026 policy changes
- **2026-04-02**: ranking change elevates "original content… created using the Snapchat camera" and shows "fewer synthetic AI videos or widely syndicated posts".
- **2026-07-31: Snapchat no longer recommends fully AI-generated videos in Spotlight.** AI tools that *enhance or edit* are still allowed.
- Growth (official): daily US Spotlight posters up >70% YoY in Q1 2026; daily posters grow followers >10x faster.
- Watermarked content reportedly suppressed (**vendor** claim).
- Safe zones (official, ads guidance): keep **top 150 px and bottom 330 px** clear of key text/logos; some agencies advise ~450 px bottom ([[Safe Zones]]).
