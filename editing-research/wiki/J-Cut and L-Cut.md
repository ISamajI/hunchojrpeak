# J-Cut and L-Cut

Split edits: the next scene's audio starts before its picture (J-cut), or the current audio trails over the next visual (L-cut), so B-roll can cover a sentence without breaking it.

## How to do it
In [[Adobe Premiere Pro]] or [[DaVinci Resolve]], unlink audio (Alt/Option-drag) and extend the audio tail or head past the video cut. Combine with continuous music and match-on-action ([[Match Cut]]).

## Why it works
Viewers leave at the "seams" between sections. Smoothing them with split edits makes the video feel like one unbroken ride.
