# Template Remix

Joining a trend by opening a ready-made edit (timing, effects, cut-outs, text, audio) and swapping in your own clips or photos. Every platform now has a native version of this mechanic.

## How to do it
- **TikTok + [[CapCut]]**: tap "CapCut – Try this template" on a trending video, or browse Templates in CapCut, swap in your media and export. **Safety note:** only get templates from the official CapCut app or capcut.com, never third-party "free template download" sites.
- **[[Edits]] (Instagram)**: open someone's template project to see how it was built (including the audio) and swap clips. Templates can lock clips (Social Media Today, 2026-07-05). Richer templates with overlays, keyframes and effects were promised (Meta, 2026-04-22), with creator collaborations (Ethan Barber, Cole Bennett). CapCut templates are still cited for Reels trends (Later, 2026-08-21).
- **Snapchat Quick Cut**: tap "use this" on someone's Quick Cut to insert your own media (see [[Beat Sync]]).
- **Pinterest collages**: make a cut-out collage Pin with a cohesive palette, enable remixing, and add a callout that it can be used as a template.

## Why it works
The barrier to joining a trend drops close to zero, and every version keeps the same pacing. Pinterest says "Pinners save collages at 2x the rate of other Pin types" (official claim).
