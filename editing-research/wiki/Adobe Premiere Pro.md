# Adobe Premiere Pro

Adobe's professional desktop NLE (Windows, macOS) with Firefly generative tools and partner models inside the timeline. Firefly (web, iPhone app) is Adobe's generative hub that routes to partner models; it is folded into this note.

## What creators use it for
[[Retention Editing]], [[J-Cut and L-Cut]], [[Zoom Punch-In]] (Scale keyframes), [[Kinetic Captions]], [[Object Mask]], [[AI B-Roll]] / Generative Extend, [[SFX Layering]], [[Long-to-Short Clipping]] (Auto Reframe).

## 2026 updates
- **v26.0** (Jan 2026, Sundance): AI Object Mask; shape masks tracking up to 20x faster; Film Impact 90+ effects and transitions; Firefly Boards import; Firefly connection to partner models (Google, OpenAI, Runway).
- **26.3**: single-word captions (per retailer blog EditorsKeys, **not Adobe release notes**).
- **v26.5** (Sept 2026): Generative Extend on 360p–4K, any aspect ratio incl. 9:16; **Generative Media** tool (Generate Video, Generate Sound Effects, Generate Soundscape beta, Generate Music beta); partner models Google Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma; new audio enhancement tools and Color Mode updates. "Paper Edit" is mentioned only by a secondary blog.
- After Effects got an AI Assistant (Sept 2026).
- Rework (July 2026, excerpt) says Generative Extend, Object Mask, Media Intelligence search and speech-to-text are included in the standard subscription; the tools note says generative features consume Firefly credits.

## Price / platforms
Paid Creative Cloud subscription; generative features use Firefly credits (exact tiers not verified). Windows, macOS.
