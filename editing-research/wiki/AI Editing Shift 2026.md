# AI Editing Shift 2026

In 2026 AI moved from separate generator apps into the editors and platforms themselves, while platforms started separating AI *editing* (accepted) from fully AI-*generated* content (increasingly demoted).

## The four shifts (from the tools note, cross-checked with platform notes)
1. **[[Sora]] is gone** (app ended 2026-04-26, API 2026-09-24). Generated-video work moved to [[Veo and Gemini Omni]], [[Kling]], [[Runway]] and [[Seedance]].
2. **Generation sits inside editors**: Seedance 2.0 in [[CapCut]]; Firefly plus Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma in [[Adobe Premiere Pro]]; Gemini Omni in YouTube Shorts and YouTube Create; still-to-video in [[Edits]]; AI Alive and AI Transitions in TikTok ([[AI B-Roll]], [[AI Video Generation]]).
3. **Chat-driven editing is its own category**: Descript Underlord, OpusClip AI Producer (ChatGPT/Claude plugin), DaVinci Resolve 21.1 with Claude and ChatGPT Codex, Captions Co-Editor, Edits AI assistant, YouTube Conversational Editing ([[Conversational AI Editing]]).
4. **Auto-dubbing with lip sync is built into platforms**: YouTube auto dubbing for all creators, Meta AI Translations free for Reels ([[AI Dubbing]]).

## The counter-movement
- Snapchat stopped recommending fully AI-generated Spotlights (2026-07-31); LinkedIn added an "AI slop" report button; YouTube cut reach for unoriginal Shorts.
- Hootsuite: nearly a third of consumers say they're less likely to choose brands using AI ads.
- Human imperfection and film grain are framed as signs of authorship ([[Lo-Fi Aesthetic]], [[Phone-First Cinematic]]; the grain claim is low-confidence).
- Rule of thumb from the raw notes: AI *editing* (captions, enhancement, cleanup) is fine; fully AI-generated posts are risky off X.

## Adoption signals (platform-reported)
YouTube: 72% of US video posters aged 14–44 used AI to create or edit in the past year; "hundreds of thousands of channels" a day used Omni in Aug 2026.
