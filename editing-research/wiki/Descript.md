# Descript

Text-based (transcript) editor for podcasts and talking-head video, with Underlord as its "AI video co-editor".

## What creators use it for
Tight [[Jump Cut]] talking heads (filler words and gaps removed), podcast-to-clips ([[Long-to-Short Clipping]]), Studio Sound cleanup, eye-contact correction, [[AI Dubbing]], [[Conversational AI Editing]] via Underlord (generates clips, writes show notes, fixes audio, does tedious edits).

## 2026 updates
Changelog releases on 2026-01-06, 02-10, 03-02, 03-17, 04-16, 05-14, 06-11, 08-05, 09-17 and 10-13 (latest "Release roundup—October 12, 2026"). One entry adds new models to Underlord's model picker; individual entries not captured (**detail unverified**).

## Price / platforms
Free: 60 media minutes/month and 100 one-time AI credits. Hobbyist: $16–$24/person/month, 400 AI credits. Creator and Business tiers above; dubbing only on Creator and up. Web and desktop (Mac/Windows).
