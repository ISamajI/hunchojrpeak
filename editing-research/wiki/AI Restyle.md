# AI Restyle

A real clip is regenerated in a new style (anime, claymation, seasonal themes), or its outfit, background or lighting is changed with a preset or a prompt.

## How to do it
- **[[Edits]]**: select a clip of 10 s or less → Restyle with Meta AI → pick a preset. Meta recommends footage with minimal fast movement.
- **Instagram Stories**: the Restyle icon in the top tray; limit of 5 videos per day.
- **[[Runway]] Aleph**: video-to-video changes to angle, lighting, objects or style.
- **[[Veo and Gemini Omni]]**: conversational edits such as "make the sky overcast, keep everything else", and "transform the world around you" remixes in Shorts.

## Why it works
It turns ordinary footage into a novel look with one tap. (The raw notes do not show a specific viral format built on it.)
