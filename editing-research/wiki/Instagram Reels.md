# Instagram Reels

Platform hub. Raw, unpolished Reels beat polished ones (New Engen, Nov 2026); most viral "edits" are one strong cut, a match transition or a text-overlay format. Short loops of ~6–15 s dominate trend templates. Meta's [[Edits]] app is now the default native editor. Status labels are the research agent's judgement, not platform metrics.

## Techniques
- **Peak**: [[Match Cut]] (leg swipe, shoe toss, repeated-action outfits) · [[Beat Sync]] · [[Loop Edit]] · [[Kinetic Captions]] · [[Rapid Fire Photo Dump]] · [[Template Remix]] · [[Lo-Fi Aesthetic]] · [[Brainrot Stack]] (per vendor; no IG-specific confirmation)
- **Rising**: [[Object Wipe Transition]] (fan transition) · [[Loading Percentage Overlay]] · [[Camera POV Reveals]] · [[Phone-First Cinematic]] · [[AI Restyle]] · [[AI Video Generation]] (AI product scenes, image-to-video) · [[Object Mask]] · [[Carousel Storytelling]] (launched 2026-10-07) · [[AI Dubbing]] (bilingual captions) · [[Zoom Punch-In]] (small text zoom)
- **Steady**: [[Split Screen]] (collage) · [[Chat Thread Video]] (fake text thread)
- **Fading / niche** (no separate notes): black-and-white-to-color reveal (peaked summer 2026; see [[Before And After Reveal]]), flicker edit (seasonal, July), blur tap-to-reveal, "Netflix documentary" mockumentary confessional (see [[Camera POV Reveals]]), sketch-to-reality transition (niche, hard to execute).
- Also rising: [[Timestamp Vlog]] (Later, 2026-10-23); fisheye effect in Edits recommended for New Engen's "And For My Final Trick" trend (Nov 2026).

## Key 2026 platform changes
- [[Edits]] shipped (2026): cinematic effects, masks + keyframes (Mar 19); bilingual captions and lockable templates (Jul 5); still-image → video and saved caption styles (Aug 20); AI assistant to all US users (Sep 30); carousel editor (Oct 7).
- Instagram re-shows carousels to returning followers from slide 2 (Social Media Today).
- Trial Reels: shown to non-followers first (public Creator/Business accounts, 200+ followers; via CreatorFlow, secondary).
- Meta AI Translations: free dubbing with optional lip sync ([[AI Dubbing]]).
- Interactive Reels (Mar 2026, details not verified).
- Business accounts: original-audio trends are not royalty-free; use Meta Sound Collection for branded or paid Reels.
- Length conflict: 6–15 s loops for trend formats vs 30–60 s having the highest reach rate (Socialinsider via CreatorFlow, Jun 2026).
- Threads / Facebook Reels: no specific editing trend found; Facebook Reels is assumed to get cross-posted IG formats (inference).
