# Seedance

ByteDance's generative video model, offered through Dreamina and inside [[CapCut]].

## Facts from the raw notes
- **Seedance 2.0 in CapCut**: text, image or reference-video to video with synced audio; clips up to 15 s in 6 aspect ratios; can edit uploaded footage. TechCrunch reported it on 2026-03-26; CapCut's newsroom post is dated 2026-04-09, with regional rollout 2026-03-25 to 2026-04-07. Safeguards: initially no generation from real faces; visible and invisible watermarks; C2PA Content Credentials.
- CapCut says it supports "rhythmic editing, with scene cuts that align well with music beats" (vendor).
- **Seedance 2.5**: the TikTok note (via SocialBee) says TikTok Symphony uses Seedance 2.5 for AI video ads (Aug 2026); Runway's changelog lists "Seedance 2.5 Draft Mode in Agent" (2026-10-02). Whether 2.5 is in CapCut is **unconfirmed**.
