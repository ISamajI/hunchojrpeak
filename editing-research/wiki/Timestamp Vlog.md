# Timestamp Vlog

A day-in-the-life Reel where each clip carries a clock timestamp text overlay, linked by swipe-right transitions, with no voiceover.

## How to do it
1. Cut 8–15 clips.
2. Add one consistent small text style showing the time in a corner. [[Edits]] now saves text styles (Social Media Today, 2026-08-20).
3. Put a slide or swipe transition between clips on beats ([[Beat Sync]]).

## Why it works
The raw note gives no explanation beyond the format. It fits the low-effort, camera-roll style of [[Rapid Fire Photo Dump]] and [[Lo-Fi Aesthetic]] (light synthesis).
