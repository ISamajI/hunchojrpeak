# Loading Percentage Overlay

Each cut raises an on-screen counter (a percentage, a battery level, a round number) until the full reveal lands at 100%.

## How to do it
1. Make one text layer per clip with the number rising (0%, 25%, … 100%).
2. Optionally add a progress-bar or battery sticker.
3. Cut on beats ([[Beat Sync]]) and save the biggest reveal for the final number.

## Why it works
The counter promises a payoff, so viewers stay to 100%.

## Examples (2026)
- TikTok: "Loading" (Later, Oct 2026), each cut raises the percentage until the full look lands.
- Instagram: "Daily GRWM loading" (Later, 2026-10-02); "Recharged" battery 1% → 100% (Later, 2026-10-30); "My reaction to ___" with an escalating number (New Engen, Nov 2026).
