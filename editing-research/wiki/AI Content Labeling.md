# AI Content Labeling

Watermarks, provenance metadata and platform labels attached to AI-generated or AI-altered video, plus the distribution rules that depend on them.

## What the raw notes record
- **CapCut / Seedance**: visible and invisible watermarks plus C2PA Content Credentials; initially no generation from real faces.
- **Gemini Omni**: SynthID watermark on all output; Shorts remixes link back to the original; creators can opt out of visual remix.
- **Meta**: dubbed reels labeled "Translated with Meta AI".
- **TikTok**: creators should use the AI-generated content setting for AI Alive and similar clips.
- **Snapchat**: fully AI-generated videos not recommended in Spotlight from 2026-07-31; AI enhancement still allowed.
- The tools note expects platforms to detect and label generated inserts (agent expectation).

## Practical rule
Label fully generated content; prefer AI for enhancement and inserts over whole posts, especially when cross-posting ([[Cross-Posting Hygiene]]).
