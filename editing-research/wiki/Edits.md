# Edits

Meta's free mobile video editor tied to Instagram Reels, built as a CapCut rival: timeline, auto-captions, templates, effects, teleprompter, green screen, cutouts, overlays and insights.

## What creators use it for
[[Template Remix]] (open a template's project to see how it was built), [[Kinetic Captions]] (auto-highlighting, per-word styling, saved styles), [[AI Dubbing]] (bilingual captions), [[Object Mask]] ("pop" segmentation effects), [[AI Restyle]], [[Carousel Storytelling]], [[Object Wipe Transition]] (masks + keyframes), [[Phone-First Cinematic]] (cinematic effects, creator filters), [[Hook AB Testing]] (multiple versions of a reel).

## 2026 timeline (from raw notes)
- **Jan**: 25 effects (bounce, fisheye, blackout), IG links in clips, weekly ideas.
- **Mar 19**: cinematic effects bundle (zoom/pan/shutter), Ethan Barber color filter, masks + keyframes, precision trim.
- **Apr 22**: one-year post; teleprompter; upcoming bilingual captions, advanced color, speed curves; templates show how they were made.
- **Jun 11–14**: Beta tab, demographic insights; AI assistant (testing) and desktop version previewed.
- **Jul 5**: bilingual captions (15 languages), lockable template clips, seasonal SFX.
- **Aug 20**: project folders, saved text/caption styles, still-image → video via prompts, audio search by theme.
- **Sep 30**: AI assistant rolled out to all US users (Net Influencer).
- **Oct 7**: carousel editor (iOS; Android "coming soon").
- **Nov**: Social Media Today homepage headline "Edits launches for MacOS" (headline only).
- Edits Plus paid tier mentioned in TikTok note.

## Vendor claims
Meta says Edits-made content gets a 10% higher save rate and 2% higher reshare rate, and more than half of Reels viewers see Edits-made content daily **[vendor claim]**.

## Conflicts
- Desktop: the tools note found **no shipping confirmation**; the TikTok note cites a Nov 2026 "Edits launches for MacOS" headline. Treat as likely but unverified.
- AI assistant: "in testing" (tools note, Jun) vs rolled out to all US users (Instagram note, Sep 30). The later report supersedes.
- Carousel editor: official steps vs a creator blog saying it sits behind a Labs toggle.

## Price / platforms
Free; iOS and Android (desktop/macOS see above).
