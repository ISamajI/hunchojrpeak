# Phone-First Cinematic

Footage shot on a phone but finished like film: slow camera moves, manual exposure, one LUT or grade across the whole piece, light grain and minimal text. "Documentary realism" is the same look with a 24 fps feel, slow zooms and captions only where needed.

## How to do it
1. Shoot on a gimbal or tripod with locked exposure.
2. Apply one LUT or grade on an adjustment layer over the whole timeline. Add grain.
3. Use slow keyframed push-ins and minimal text. Optionally animate [[Letterboxing]] bars in before a big moment (AIR).
- **Tools**: [[DaVinci Resolve]] (free) or [[Final Cut Pro]] for heavier grades (OpusClip's recommendation); [[Edits]] filters, the Ethan Barber creator color filter and the cinematic effects bundle (zoom / pan / shutter, Social Media Today 2026-03-19), plus advanced color adjustments listed as upcoming (2026-04-22); [[Premiere Mobile]] .cube LUT import (April 2026). Digicam / vintage looks from VSCO or Dazz Cam (low-confidence source).

## Why it works
It stands out against fast-cut and raw feeds ("this is not made for the algorithm") and signals quality within about 2 s. A photography-trend site claims visible grain reads as "human authorship" in an AI-saturated feed and that film-grain searches rose 31% (**unverified, low confidence**).
