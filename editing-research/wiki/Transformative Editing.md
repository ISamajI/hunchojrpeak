# Transformative Editing

Reposting or building on others' footage only with clear original commentary, "unique edits" or storytelling added. In 2026 several platforms began paying for originality and demoting aggregator re-uploads, which turns transformative editing from good practice into a distribution requirement.

## Platform rules (2026)
- **YouTube, 2026-10-01**: channels that "primarily aggregat[e] or re-upload… other creators' videos without significant changes will likely see less distribution within the Shorts feed."
- **X, Nov 2026**: Video Kickstart Bonus and Original Content Rewards revenue share, designed to "disincentivize aggregators"; your own cross-posts are allowed ([[Native Vertical Video]]).
- **Snapchat, 2026-04-02**: "fewer synthetic AI videos or widely syndicated posts"; from 2026-07-31 fully AI-generated videos are not recommended.
- **Clip economy**: CreatorDB warns platforms are "deciding how much recycled content their feeds can hold" ([[Stream Clip Highlights]]).

## How to edit transformatively
- Add commentary on top of the source ([[Green Screen Cutout]] greenscreen explain counts as "adding value").
- Re-edit rather than repost: new hook, new structure, new captions ([[Cross-Posting Hygiene]]).
- Avoid others' footage as filler in a [[Brainrot Stack]].
