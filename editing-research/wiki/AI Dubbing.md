# AI Dubbing

Translated voice tracks that keep the creator's voice and emotion, increasingly with lip sync, plus the caption-side equivalent: auto-translated bilingual captions.

## How to do it
- **[[YouTube]] auto dubbing**: free, open to all creators since 2026-02-04, 27 languages; Expressive Speech keeps emotion and energy; a Lip Sync pilot is testing; smart filtering skips content like music videos.
- **Meta AI Translations**: free dubbing with optional lip sync for Reels. Facebook: creators with 1,000+ followers; Instagram: all public accounts where Meta AI is available. Dubbed reels are labeled "Translated with Meta AI". (Meta post dates to 2025-10, updated 2026-01-20; partly stale.)
- **[[Edits]] bilingual captions**: Captions → bilingual / auto-translate, 15 languages (shipped ~2026-07-05).
- **ElevenLabs** Dubbing v2 (90+ languages, date unverified); **[[Captions]]** (29 dubbing languages); **[[Descript]]** (Creator plan and up).

## Why it works
Global reach for talking-head and explainer channels without re-recording.

## Platforms
[[YouTube]] · [[Instagram Reels]] (and Facebook Reels)

## Status
Rising. Bilingual and translated captions are rising alongside auto-dubbing.
