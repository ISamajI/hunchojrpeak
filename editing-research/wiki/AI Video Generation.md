# AI Video Generation

Clips created by a generative model from text, a still image or reference video: animating still photos, AI product scenes, or fully AI-made clips. Generated *inserts* inside a real edit are covered in [[AI B-Roll]].

## How to do it (native tools, 2026)
- **TikTok**: AI Alive (camera → AI Alive side-panel button → prompt; Stories) turns a photo into video. Image-to-Video, Text-to-Video and an "AI Transition" tool were added Nov 2025; AI Transitions in the clip editor May 2026 (no viral trend built on it found yet). **Label it** with TikTok's AI-generated content setting.
- **[[CapCut]]**: [[Seedance]] 2.0 (clips up to 15 s, six aspect ratios).
- **[[Edits]]**: conversational prompts that turn a still into an animated clip (Social Media Today, 2026-08-20).
- **X**: Grok Imagine tab, vertical aspect; multi-image input (Mar 2026). "Extend" chaining in 6–10 s steps is described only by third-party guides (unverified). Use it for B-roll or inserts rather than whole posts if you plan to cross-post.
- **YouTube**: [[Veo and Gemini Omni]] in Shorts Remix and the YouTube Create app.
- **Standalone**: [[Kling]] (strong human motion; animating old photos, memes, product shots), [[Runway]]. [[Sora]] is discontinued.

## Why it works
It makes new visuals from nothing and fuels "me vs. AI" comparisons, animated story series ([[Episodic Series]]) and AI product scenes ("AI tipping scene", Instagram, Later 2026-09-18).

## Backlash and policy
- [[Snapchat Spotlight]]: from **2026-07-31, fully AI-generated videos are ineligible for Spotlight recommendations**. AI tools that enhance or edit are still fine.
- Hootsuite: nearly a third of consumers say they're less likely to choose brands that use AI ads.
- [[LinkedIn]] added an "AI slop" report button; YouTube is cracking down on unoriginal content.
- See [[AI Content Labeling]] for watermarks (SynthID, C2PA).
