# Loop Edit

A very short video (about 6–15 s) whose last frame flows back into the first, so autoplay re-loops count as rewatches.

## How to do it
1. Keep it to 6–15 s. New Engen specifies this length on nearly every November 2026 Instagram trend.
2. End on a frame or motion that matches the opening.
3. Leave on-screen text up long enough to read on the first pass.
- Pinterest variant: open on the result, show the process, and end on the result again ([[Payoff-First Hook]]).

## Why it works
Rewatches from looping add watch time on short trend formats.

## Conflicting signal
Socialinsider data cited by CreatorFlow (secondary, June 2026) says **30–60 s Reels had the highest reach rate**. So short loops serve trend formats while longer cuts serve reach. Treat length as format-dependent.
