# Retention Editing

Shaping the edit around the YouTube Studio audience-retention graph instead of the order things were shot: cold opens, payoffs previewed early, rehooks where viewers tend to drop, and no "winding down" energy at the end.

## How to do it (any NLE: Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut)
1. Before editing, review the retention graphs of your last 5 videos and classify the drops: early crash, slow mid-video decline, sudden cliff, or end falloff.
2. Open with a highlights teaser (under 10 s), a direct value statement, or a cold open ("but let me back up").
3. Change something visual every 10–15 s in the opening and put the first deliberate pattern interrupt at about 25–35 s.
4. Rehook about every 2–3 min ([[Rehook]]).
5. Cut finalizing language ("to recap", "lastly"), move the CTA to mid-video (under 5 s), and keep talking over the end screen.
6. Do a "rewatch test" the next day with your phone out of reach, and fix every spot where you feel the urge to check it.
- Test openings with [[Hook AB Testing]] once available.

## Why it works
YouTube Studio's retention report includes an "Intro" metric (share still watching after 30 s), and videos above 50% are flagged as outperforming. vidIQ (cited by Pixflow) says most drop-off, often 55%+, happens in the first minute.
