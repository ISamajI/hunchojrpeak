# Brainrot Stack

A maximalist vertical collage: 2–3 stacked layers (a talking head on top, gameplay such as Subway Surfers or satisfying B-roll below, plus an ASMR sound layer), cuts every **0.6–0.8 s**, and three-color [[Kinetic Captions]] jumping by phrase.

## How to do it
1. In [[CapCut]] or Premiere, use a 9:16 split vertical canvas.
2. Place the gameplay or satisfying B-roll on the bottom track and the talking head on top.
3. Add a low-volume ASMR or gameplay audio bed.
4. Use aggressive [[Jump Cut]]s and kinetic captions. Tools: CapCut, [[Opus Clip]], [[Submagic]].

## Why it works
Sensory overload: more inputs make it harder to scroll away.

## Risk on YouTube
YouTube's **2026-10-01** Shorts update reduces reach for re-uploaded content without original contribution. Using someone else's footage as filler or as the top layer is risky unless you add real commentary or editing. See [[Transformative Editing]].
