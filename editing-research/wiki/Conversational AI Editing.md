# Conversational AI Editing

Editing by chatting with an AI assistant: you prompt a first draft, then keep giving natural-language instructions ("trim pauses", "reorder for better pacing", "sync music to the beat"), switching to manual timeline edits when you want.

## Tools (2026)
- **YouTube**: Gemini-powered Conversational Editing in the Shorts composer and YouTube Create app (**previewed** 2026-09-23; full availability unconfirmed). Gemini Omni was added to Shorts Remix and YouTube Create on 2026-05-19 ([[Veo and Gemini Omni]]).
- **[[Descript]]** Underlord ("AI video co-editor").
- **[[Opus Clip]]** AI Producer, a plugin for ChatGPT or Claude that edits footage to match a style you show it.
- **[[DaVinci Resolve]] 21.1** works with Claude, Claude Code and ChatGPT Codex to analyze projects, organize media, batch render and "create highlight edits".
- **[[Captions]]** Co-Editor; **[[Runway]]** Aleph (video-to-video) and Agent.
- **[[Edits]]** AI assistant (in testing Jun 2026; reportedly rolled out to all US users ~2026-09-30 per Net Influencer).
- **[[CapCut]]** tested "Edit Pilot" / "AI Agent Editing" (Nov 2025); a ChatGPT plugin is "coming soon" (Nov 2026).
- YouTube Studio "Ask Studio" gives draft feedback on pacing and structure.

## Why it matters
It lowers the skill floor for complex edits. YouTube says "hundreds of thousands of channels" a day used Omni in Aug 2026, and its GenAI trends report says 72% of US video posters aged 14–44 used AI to create or edit in the past year (platform-reported).
