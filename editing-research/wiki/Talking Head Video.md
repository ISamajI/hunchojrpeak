# Talking Head Video

One person speaking to a single vertical camera with minimal production: one shot, one angle, usually no B-roll (or one cutaway in the middle), and captions.

## How to do it
- **YouTube Shorts "talking-head static"** (OpusClip): one shot, one angle, no B-roll; captions slightly off-center and hard-cut by paragraph.
- **LinkedIn expert-POV version**: 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Hook in the first line with a business outcome, not a meme hook. Burned-in captions (bold sans-serif, white/yellow with dark stroke, ≤42 characters/line, lower third above the buttons; vendor guidance). Use a lav mic. Open and close on the face, with optional B-roll in the middle.
- **Instagram**: part of the [[Lo-Fi Aesthetic]]: one take, trim, one text hook, auto-captions. [[Edits]] has a teleprompter.
- Tidy pauses with [[Jump Cut]]s or [[Descript]], but keep natural pacing.

## Why it works
Trust beats production value when the person is the brand, and faces build trust while AI text floods feeds. LinkedIn's Creator Video newsletter (May 2026) says the best videos are "grounded in real experience and a clear point of view" and recommends 2–5 posts per week, 2 of them video.
- Vendor / unverifiable claims: OpusClip says the format "built dozens of $1M-ARR creators in 2026"; LinkedIn personal profiles get 65% vs 5% feed share for company pages (vendor-cited).
