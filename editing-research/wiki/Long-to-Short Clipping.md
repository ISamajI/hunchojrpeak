# Long-to-Short Clipping

Cutting long-form content (podcasts, webinars, interviews, streams) into short vertical clips, increasingly with AI that ranks candidate clips and auto-reframes 16:9 to 9:16.

## How to do it
- **Manual (LinkedIn-first)**: pull the strongest 30–60 s, re-write the opening line for the audience ("reframe, don't repost"), use multi-cam speaker switching, add captions and a business-outcome title card, end with a business-outcome CTA.
- **AI tools**:
  - [[Opus Clip]]: Virality Score (0–99); 4.0 adds Auto-edit, Recipes, Viral Hook templates.
  - [[Descript]] Underlord; [[Captions]] Long-to-Short and Clips Chat; [[Submagic]] Magic Clips (8 credits each).
  - [[DaVinci Resolve]] 21.1 with an AI assistant to "create highlight edits".
  - TikTok Studio **Smart Split** (auto-clips long videos with captions and vertical reframing; it crops rather than truly reframes; rolled out from Oct 2025).
- **Auto Reframe** (subject-tracking 16:9 → 9:16 / 1:1): [[Premiere Mobile]] (one tap, May 2026), [[Adobe Premiere Pro]], [[Opus Clip]], [[CapCut]], [[DaVinci Resolve]] Studio Smart Reframe (not re-verified for v21).

## Why it works
Per Wistia/HubSpot State of Video 2026 (cited by Rework, **vendor-cited secondary**), over half of companies repurpose long-form into short clips and **67% of those post the clips to LinkedIn first**. Native video also avoids LinkedIn's outbound-link reach penalty.

## Caveat
Pure clipping of other people's content risks YouTube's unoriginal-content cut ([[Transformative Editing]]).
