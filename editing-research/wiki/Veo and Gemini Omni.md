# Veo and Gemini Omni

Google's generative video models. Gemini Omni (Omni Flash introduced at Google I/O, 2026-05-19) does generative video with native audio and conversational video editing; Veo continues for developers (Veo 3.1, per a secondary podcast source) and as a partner model inside Premiere.

## What creators use it for
- AI remixes of trending Shorts (Shorts Remix) and the YouTube Create app ([[Conversational AI Editing]]).
- Conversational edits of real footage ("make the sky overcast, keep everything else"; "transform the world around you") ([[AI Restyle]]).
- Avatar videos in your own voice; physics gag videos ([[AI Video Generation]]).
- [[AI B-Roll]] inside [[Adobe Premiere Pro]] (Veo as partner model).

## Facts from the raw notes
- Gemini Omni added to Shorts Remix and YouTube Create on 2026-05-19.
- YouTube says "hundreds of thousands of channels" a day used it in Aug 2026 (platform-reported).
- All output carries a **SynthID** watermark; Omni remixes link back to the original; creators can opt out of visual remix ([[AI Content Labeling]]).
- Conversational Editing (Gemini-powered) previewed 2026-09-23; full availability unconfirmed.

## Price / platforms
Free in YouTube Shorts and YouTube Create; in the Gemini app and Flow for Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers; API for developers; free 1080p in Google Vids.
