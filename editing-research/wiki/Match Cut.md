# Match Cut

Two clips are joined on matching motion, gesture or framing, so the cut reads as one continuous action while the outfit, location or time changes.

## How to do it
1. Lock the phone on a tripod and keep the framing identical across takes.
2. End take A mid-motion (leg crossing the lens, shoe leaving the hand, stepping down a stair). Start take B on the same motion.
3. Cut at the motion peak, where the movement covers most of the frame (CapCut: split the clip at that frame).
4. Keep the cut hard, or add a short blur, whip or zoom. Edits added transition pace and speed controls (Instagram post, date unverified).
5. Put the cut on a beat of the trending audio (see [[Beat Sync]]).
- **Long-form version** (Pixflow / AIR): match-on-action and shot-size progression (skip a size, e.g. wide → medium-close). Keep the subject within about 10% of its previous position in the frame.

## Why it works
The motion carries across the cut, so the video feels like one shot and has a loop-like pull. Viewers rewatch to find the cut. On long-form, smoothing the "seams" between sections stops people leaving at them.

- TikTok: "Stairs" (Aug 2026, endless descent through stairs, trap doors, couches); "Upside down" (Oct 2026, hand gesture triggers a before/after).
