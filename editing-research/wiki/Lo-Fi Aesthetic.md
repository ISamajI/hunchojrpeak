# Lo-Fi Aesthetic

Deliberately under-edited video: one take or a few, natural pacing, small stumbles left in, light trims, minimal grading and few effects. On Snapchat this means shooting in the Snapchat camera with minimal post-production.

## How to do it
1. Shoot in-app or on the phone camera, handheld and in natural light.
2. Trim the head and tail. Maybe apply a light auto-enhance. Skip heavy effects stacks.
3. Add one text hook in the first 3 s, then auto-captions (Instagram note: "That's it.").
4. Pair with [[Text Overlay POV]] for context, or use a [[Talking Head Video]] with no B-roll.
- **Snapchat**: shoot in the Snapchat camera, use Lenses / AR as the effect layer, avoid obviously recycled TikTok or Reels uploads, strip watermarks (Spotlight reportedly suppresses watermarked content; vendor claim), and post daily.

## Why it works
- Hootsuite Social Trends 2026: "Over-editing is out, and the occasional stutter or flub is in… Imperfections, natural pacing — even typos! — signal authenticity."
- New Engen puts "raw, unpolished content outperforming high-production posts" first on its 2026 Instagram list. CreatorFlow: "Stop spending 3 hours editing a 30-second Reel."
- It sets human content apart from the flood of AI content.
- Snap's ranking change (2026-04-02) explicitly favours "original content… created using the Snapchat camera" and shows "fewer synthetic AI videos or widely syndicated posts". Snap says daily US Spotlight posters grew >70% YoY in Q1 2026 and daily posters gain followers >10x faster (official figures).
