# Chat Thread Video

A video carried by a chat conversation: either a real screen recording of a chatbot or text thread, or mock message bubbles popped in over B-roll.

## How to do it
- **Screen recording (TikTok)**: use the phone's screen recorder, trim in TikTok, optionally zoom in on the answers and add text.
- **Mock thread overlay (Instagram)**: build chat bubbles as image overlays or text-box stickers, and pop each one in on a beat over ~7 s of B-roll ([[Beat Sync]]).

## Why it works
The reading rhythm creates suspense line by line and works on mute. (The raw notes give no further explanation; this is a light synthesis.)

## Examples (2026)
- TikTok: "Answer in One Word" (Nov 2026), asking ChatGPT, friends or family personal questions with one-word answers.
- Instagram: "Where are you" → "Home" → "Your location says…" (Later, 2026-09-18); "Let me guess" with screenshot "receipts" (2026-08-14).
