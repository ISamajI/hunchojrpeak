# X

Platform hub. X is pushing native, original vertical video with money and a refreshed AI-assisted composer, while stan-account fan edits remain its distinctive edit culture.

## Techniques
- **Rising**: [[Native Vertical Video]] (paid via Kickstart) · [[Kinetic Captions]] (Grok-edited captions in the composer) · [[Green Screen Cutout]] (native green screen, adoption unverified)
- **Peak**: [[Fan Edits]] (saturating; X-specific evidence is cultural, not metric) · [[Speed Ramp]] (velocity edits)
- **Peak on X / fading elsewhere**: [[AI Video Generation]] via Grok Imagine

## Key 2026 platform changes
- **Video Kickstart Bonus** (invite-only pilot, launched Nov 2026; reported 2026-11-01): pays creators who post **15 qualifying vertical videos on 15 different days** in ~1 month. Videos must be original but may be your own cross-posts. Participants are fast-tracked into Original Content Rewards revenue share, designed to "disincentivize aggregators".
- Editor timeline: Mar 2026 Grok multi-image video input; Jul 2026 editor update (multi-language captions + green screen recording); 2026-08-16 text/emoji video overlays (iOS); 2026-10-04 composer refresh with Grok caption editing.
- Specs: 9:16 1080×1920 for Kickstart (<3 min); organic 140 s (longer with Premium). No official safe zone; keep text off the bottom ~15% (inference). See [[Safe Zones]].
