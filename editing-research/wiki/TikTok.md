# TikTok

Platform hub. TikTok trends in Oct–Nov 2026 are dominated by low-effort, text-led and template-led formats, with transitions and reveals as evergreen formats. Trend evidence is from weekly trackers (New Engen, Later, SocialBee), which describe formats, not technique usage; **no source gave hard usage numbers for any technique**.

## Techniques
- **Peak**: [[Text Overlay POV]] (most common shape Oct–Nov 2026) · [[Match Cut]] · [[Beat Sync]] · [[Before And After Reveal]] · [[Speed Ramp]] · [[Green Screen Cutout]] · [[Template Remix]] · [[Split Screen]] · [[Lo-Fi Aesthetic]] · [[Kinetic Captions]] (saturated)
- **Rising**: [[Object Wipe Transition]] (Fan Transition) · [[Cinematic Slow Motion]] · [[Carousel Storytelling]] (volume up, reach lower) · [[Episodic Series]] · [[Chat Thread Video]] · [[Loading Percentage Overlay]] · [[AI Video Generation]] (with backlash)
- **Mature / possibly fading**: [[Jump Cut]] as a deliberate style (unverified)

## Native tools and 2026 changes
- TikTok editor / TikTok Studio: stack, trim, split, overlays (PiP), speed, SFX, text timing; Green Screen, Cutout (+Stroke, Nov 2025), Mask (Oct 2025).
- Collage for photo posts (Jan 2026); "Create with AI" for Effects (Mar 2026); AI Transitions in the clip editor (May 2026; no viral trend found); "AI Pick for Me" test (Jun 2026); desktop photo posts and voice typing for photo posts (Jul 2026).
- AI Alive (photo → video in Stories) and Image/Text-to-Video (Nov 2025).
- **Smart Split** in TikTok Studio Web auto-clips long videos with captions and vertical reframing (crop, not true reframe), from the Oct 2025 Creator Summit ([[Long-to-Short Clipping]]).
- Advertisers: Symphony Creative Studio "Reference to Video" (TikTok World '26) and Seedance 2.5 for AI video ads (Aug 2026, via SocialBee).
- TikTok's October 2026 discovery summary names "text-led posts" as one of four main clusters (snippet only).
- Metricool (2026-05-12, 2.3M posts): carousels up ~140% YoY but videos get 5x the reach and 6x the interactions.
- US: TikTok USDS Joint Venture (deal close 2026-01-22 per secondary sources) also covers [[CapCut]].
