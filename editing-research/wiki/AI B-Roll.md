# AI B-Roll

Generated or auto-inserted cutaways inside a real edit, used to cover jump cuts, illustrate what is being said, or extend a clip that is a few frames short. (Whole AI-made posts are covered in [[AI Video Generation]].)

## How to do it
- **[[Adobe Premiere Pro]] 26.5** (Sept 2026) Generative Media tool: Generate Video fills timeline gaps using project reference frames; models are Firefly plus partners (Google Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma). Also Generate Sound Effects, Soundscape (beta) and Music (beta).
- **Generative Extend** (Premiere): adds frames to a clip's head or tail to hold a beat or fix short handles; now 360p–4K, any aspect ratio including 9:16. Uses Firefly credits.
- **[[CapCut]]** with [[Seedance]]; **[[Opus Clip]]** Auto-edit; **[[Captions]]** AI Edit styles; **[[Submagic]]** auto B-roll.
- Label generated inserts where platforms require ([[AI Content Labeling]]).

## Why it works
It removes the need to shoot or license cover footage for talking-head content.
