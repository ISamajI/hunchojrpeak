# Native Vertical Video

Shooting and exporting 9:16 (1080×1920) and uploading natively to each platform, rather than linking out or posting 16:9 crops. In 2026 this spread beyond TikTok/Reels/Shorts to X and LinkedIn.

## How to do it
- **X**: export 1080×1920, under 3:00, post daily, remove other platforms' watermarks, and put the hook in frame 1 (X autoplays muted). The **Video Kickstart Bonus** (invite-only pilot, Nov 2026) pays creators who post **15 qualifying vertical videos on 15 different days** in about a month; videos must be original but may be your own cross-posts.
- **LinkedIn**: 9:16 for 30–90 s discovery clips; keep 16:9 or 4:5 for 2–5 min educational pieces for warm audiences, and A/B test.
- General: see [[Safe Zones]] and [[Cross-Posting Hygiene]].

## Why it works
- X now pays for it, and fast-tracks Kickstart participants into Original Content Rewards revenue share, designed to "disincentivize aggregators" (official, via Social Media Today).
- LinkedIn: vendors report a distribution boost for vertical in the short-form feed **[vendor]**. **Conflicting evidence:** one LinkedIn creator reports horizontal outperforming vertical by ~10x at ~2 min **[unverified anecdote]**.
