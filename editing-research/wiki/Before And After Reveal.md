# Before And After Reveal

A then-to-now or start-to-finish transformation, cut to the "after" on the audio drop or key word, often with the cut hidden by a transition.

## How to do it
1. Collect the "before" media (old photos or clips).
2. Cut to the "after" on the drop or key word of the audio ([[Beat Sync]]).
3. Keep the transition simple, or hide the cut with a [[Match Cut]] or [[Object Wipe Transition]].
- Variants: counters that climb to the final look ([[Loading Percentage Overlay]]); Instagram's "It's Time" (before → after on the line); a black-and-white-to-color reveal (Instagram, summer 2026, likely fading).

## Why it works
The end payoff gives people a reason to finish the video. New Engen lists it as one of five evergreen 2026 formats.

## Examples (2026)
- TikTok, late Oct 2026: "Just take it back to the beginning" (old photos next to today) and "I just want to make a lot of money" (first day at a job, then success now).

[[TikTok]] · [[Instagram Reels]]. On [[Pinterest]] the logic is reversed: open on the result ([[Payoff-First Hook]]).
