# Episodic Series

Short vertical stories released as numbered parts or episodes, each ending on a cliffhanger or a question so viewers come back for the next one.

## How to do it
- **TikTok story parts**: script short daily beats, record a voiceover over AI animation, stick-figure animation or illustrations, add an on-screen "Part N" label and an end question. Label AI content ([[AI Content Labeling]]).
- **YouTube Shorts Series / microdramas**: scripted 30 s–3 min episodes grouped into seasons with custom thumbnails and sequential playback. Each episode needs a 1–2 s cold-open recap and a hard cliffhanger cut at the end. Keep the grade and caption style consistent across the series.

## Why it works
It is built for bingeing. In the TikTok "Zombie Outbreak Survival Stories" trend, comments request new locations, so the location is the hook. beCreatives also notes multi-scene, multi-part storytelling replacing single takes.

## Examples
"Zombie Outbreak Survival Stories" (TikTok, Nov 2026): second-person narration ("Day 1. You're stuck in…").
