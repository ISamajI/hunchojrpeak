# Cross-Posting Hygiene

Rules for reusing one edit across platforms without being treated as a syndicated repost.

## The 2026 rules (Other Platforms note)
1. **Edit once vertically, re-edit per platform.** Same footage, but rewrite the hook, caption tone and CTA: LinkedIn = business outcome; X = news/opinion; Pinterest = result/search keyword; Snap = candid (Visla FAQ, Contentin.io; vendor).
2. **Strip watermarks** before cross-posting. Snap reportedly suppresses syndicated/watermarked content (vendor claim); X rewards originals but explicitly allows *your own* cross-posts.
3. **Fully AI-generated = risky** on Snapchat and increasingly elsewhere; AI editing is fine ([[AI Content Labeling]]).
4. **Burn in captions** for muted autoplay and keep them inside [[Safe Zones]].
- Instagram note: Edits shares directly to Instagram; Facebook Reels is assumed to receive cross-posted IG formats (inference).
