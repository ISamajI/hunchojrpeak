# YouTube

Platform hub covering Shorts and long-form. Long-form is moving away from non-stop stimulation toward **deliberately varied pacing**; Shorts has split into many recognizable aesthetics, with both maximalist and minimal extremes performing. Most "trend" sources here are vendor or agency blogs (OpusClip, Pixflow, AIR Media-Tech), so their numbers are directional.

## Techniques
- **Long-form, peak**: [[Retention Editing]] · [[Rehook]] · [[J-Cut and L-Cut]] · [[SFX Layering]] · [[Video Essay Style]] · [[Zoom Punch-In]] (fatigue risk)
- **Long-form, rising**: [[Variable Pacing]] (incl. post-MrBeast story-first slowdown) · [[Hook AB Testing]]
- **Shorts, peak**: [[Kinetic Captions]] (fading as a differentiator — agent inference) · [[Brainrot Stack]] (at risk from originality update) · [[Green Screen Cutout]] (greenscreen explain)
- **Shorts, rising**: [[Talking Head Video]] · [[Phone-First Cinematic]] (documentary realism) · [[Episodic Series]] (Shorts Series, microdramas) · [[Transformative Editing]] · [[Conversational AI Editing]]
- Vendor-only, unverified popularity: hands-only ASMR and audio-reactive AI edits (OpusClip).
- Fading / at risk: uniform "overstim" cutting on long-form; a zoom and whoosh on every sentence; long branded intros and "hey guys" openers; aggregator re-uploads on Shorts.

## Key 2026 platform and policy changes
- **2026-10-01: Shorts unoriginal-content reach cut.** Channels that "primarily aggregat[e] or re-upload… other creators' videos without significant changes will likely see less distribution within the Shorts feed." See [[Transformative Editing]].
- **2026-05-19 (I/O)**: Gemini Omni in Shorts Remix and YouTube Create ([[Veo and Gemini Omni]]); SynthID watermarks; creators can opt out of visual remix.
- **2026-09-23 (Made on YouTube)**: Conversational Editing previewed; Studio video A/B testing of up to 3 cuts "coming soon"; Ask Studio draft feedback; channel-matched AI thumbnails and dynamic thumbnails; Shorts Series; microdrama subscriptions after the event.
- **2026-02-04**: auto dubbing open to all creators, 27 languages ([[AI Dubbing]]).
- YouTube GenAI trends report: 72% of US video posters aged 14–44 used AI to create or edit in the past year (platform-reported, not opened).
