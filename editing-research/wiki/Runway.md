# Runway

Generative video platform: generates video (Gen-4.5), edits or transforms existing video (Aleph), and now also serves third-party models.

## What creators use it for
Video-to-video restyles (angle, lighting, objects, style) ([[AI Restyle]]); cinematic AI shots ([[AI Video Generation]]); background removal ([[Object Mask]]); AI SFX; ad creatives; [[AI B-Roll]] inside [[Adobe Premiere Pro]] (partner model).

## 2026 changelog
Runway Ads (2026-10-27); App Studio (10-26); Mirelo SFX 2.0 (10-15); BiRefNet v2 background removal (10-15); Fish Audio Drama 3 (10-14); Seedream 5.0 Flash (10-12); project credit caps (10-09); Seedance 2.5 Draft Mode in Agent (10-02). The Gen-4 research page mentions "Aleph 2.0" (details unverified).

## Price / platforms
Freemium, credit-based; Aleph on all paid plans. Web, iOS, API.
