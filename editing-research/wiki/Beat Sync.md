# Beat Sync

Cuts, reveals, object placements or text land exactly on the beats or cue words of the audio. It ranges from manual cut-on-the-beat editing to auto-cut montages such as Snapchat's Quick Cut.

## How to do it
- **[[CapCut]]**: add the track, use beat markers (auto beat detection where offered), and split clips at each marker.
- **[[Premiere Mobile]]**: Beat Detection (added April 2026).
- **[[Edits]] / Reels**: save the audio from the Reels audio page (Edits' Ideas tab stores saved audio), mark beats, and put the hard cut or reveal on the cue frame. Keep it 6–15 s so it loops (see [[Loop Edit]]).
- **Snapchat Quick Cut**: Memories or Camera Roll → select clips → Quick Cut. Swap the Lens or the Sound pill, or tap "use this" on someone else's Quick Cut to remix it.
- **Long-form ("cut to the beat")**: land hard cuts on kicks or snares (AIR). Don't put a pop on every subtitle (see [[SFX Layering]]).
- **Licensing**: business accounts should use TikTok's Commercial Music Library or Meta's Sound Collection. Later notes that "original audio" trends on Instagram are not royalty-free.

## Why it works
It is satisfying to watch and pulls the viewer toward a payoff. New Engen describes trending audio as "a cultural signal — not just a distribution mechanic." Snap's Quick Cut makes it zero-skill and native, and the remix mechanic spreads formats.

## Examples (2026)
- TikTok: "What do you bring to the table?" (Sept 2026, bag items placed one per beat); "Loading" (Oct 2026, see [[Loading Percentage Overlay]]).
- Instagram: "It's Time" (cut before→after on the line, 7–12 s), "War Is Over", "The Nicole Kidman Era", "On-off" / "Toggle on" (Later, 2026-08-07 and 2026-10-30).
- Snapchat: Quick Cut montages (launched 2025-12-17, slightly before the research window but still current).
