# Sora

OpenAI's video generation app and API. **Discontinued.**

## Status
- OpenAI announced the shutdown on 2026-03-24.
- Sora web and app experiences ended **2026-04-26**; the API ended **2026-09-24** (OpenAI Help Center).
- TechCrunch, citing WSJ: users peaked around 1M, fell below 500K, and the app cost about $1M/day to run.

## Relevance
Sora 2 "cameo" (later "characters") deepfake edits were a late-2025 viral format. Anything still labeled "Sora" is legacy or a re-upload. Generated-video workflows have moved to [[Veo and Gemini Omni]], [[Kling]], [[Runway]] and [[Seedance]].
