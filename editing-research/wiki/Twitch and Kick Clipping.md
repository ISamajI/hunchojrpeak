# Twitch and Kick Clipping

Platform hub for stream clip culture. Twitch and Kick are mainly *sources* of clips that are edited and distributed on TikTok, Shorts and Reels, increasingly through paid clipper networks. No Kick-specific editing tools were found; Kick's role is as a funder of clipping.

## Techniques
- **Peak**: [[Stream Clip Highlights]] (paid clipping, saturation/regulation risk)
- **Rising**: [[Facecam Stack Layout]] (becoming default with Dual Format) · stream recaps (inside [[Stream Clip Highlights]]; native Recaps Feed announced, not shipped)
- Supporting: [[Kinetic Captions]] · [[Long-to-Short Clipping]]
- Fading: center-cropped 16:9 stream clips

## Key 2026 changes
- **Twitch Dual Format** GA 2026-06-17: separate custom vertical and horizontal layouts streamed at once; ~70% of new viewers arrive on mobile (Twitch ANZ).
- Twitch auto-generates portrait clips that keep cam and gameplay in frame.
- Twitch Recaps Feed announced at Tokyo Game Show 2026 (no ship date).
- Clip economy figures: N3on paid >$1.4M to 303 clippers in 5 weeks (Apr 2026); CreatorDB: 9,941 clips → 1.68B YouTube views + 260M TikTok plays in 30 days for six streamers (data vendor).
- Voice-triggered clipping reported only in a social post (unverified).
