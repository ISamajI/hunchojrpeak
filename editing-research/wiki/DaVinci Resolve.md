# DaVinci Resolve

Blackmagic Design's editor with color, Fusion VFX and Fairlight audio. The free version is popular with YouTubers and cinematic editors.

## What creators use it for
[[Phone-First Cinematic]] / documentary-realism grades, Speed Warp slow motion ([[Speed Ramp]]), Relight and depth-map effects, Fusion motion graphics, multi-cam podcast and event edits ([[Long-to-Short Clipping]]), [[Zoom Punch-In]] (Dynamic Zoom), [[SFX Layering]] (Fairlight).

## 2026 updates
- **Resolve 21**: new Photo page; AI IntelliSearch (search media by content); slate reading, de-aging, blemish removal; AI Speech Generator (secondary: Facebook group summary); faster Relight, Depth Map and Speed Warp; better keyframing on Edit and Cut pages; Fairlight folders.
- **Resolve 21.1** (IBC, 2026-09-08): works with AI assistants (Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT Codex) to analyze projects, organize media, batch render and "create highlight edits" ([[Conversational AI Editing]]); 25-angle multi-cam shortcuts; inspector presets; new log formats (GoPro, Leica, DJI D-Log2, Vivo); 25 new Krokodove Fusion tools.

## Price / platforms
Resolve 21 free; Resolve Studio 21 is a paid one-time purchase that adds DaVinci Neural Engine AI tools (a Reddit summary says AI features are Studio-only). The commonly cited $295 Studio price is **unverified**. Windows, macOS, Linux; iPad version (2026 status not re-verified).
