# Opus Clip

Web tool that turns long videos (podcasts, streams, webinars) into vertical short clips, each with a "Virality Score" (0–99). OpusClip's blog is also a heavily cited — and **vendor-biased** — source for 2026 "aesthetics" claims in this vault.

## What creators use it for
[[Long-to-Short Clipping]], [[Kinetic Captions]] (emoji and keyword highlight), Viral Hook templates, [[AI B-Roll]], motion graphics for [[Video Essay Style]], [[Brainrot Stack]] captions.

## 2026 updates
**OpusClip 4.0** (2026-11-02): Auto-edit for talking videos (speech cleanup, captions, B-roll); every format from one edit (60–90 s highlights, 3–5 min cuts, lo-fi updates); Recipes (saved setups); Viral Hook templates; Motion Graphics; **AI Producer**, a plugin for ChatGPT or Claude that edits footage to match a style you show it ([[Conversational AI Editing]]). Changelog also lists Motion Studio, Speech Enhancement sliders, and direct publishing to Pinterest and YouTube (entry dates not captured).

## Price / platforms
Freemium; Pro is paid (the 4.0 post promotes 40% off the first month; full tiers not verified). Web; mobile app is mainly for review and posting (secondary).

## Bias note
Claims such as brainrot being "the dominant short-form aesthetic of 2026" and talking-head static having "built dozens of $1M-ARR creators" come from OpusClip's own blog and are **unverified**.
