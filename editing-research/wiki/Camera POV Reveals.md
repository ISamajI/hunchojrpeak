# Camera POV Reveals

A family of in-camera moves that stop the scroll without effects. New Engen's November 2026 list groups them as "camera POV innovation".

## The moves
- **Knee-to-face**: one slow, continuous rise, slower than feels natural, with a pause at the top.
- **Close-up-to-wide**: open on a detail, then pull back within about 2–5 s. It creates a "micro-mystery"; New Engen says completion rates are high (not independently measured).
- **Overhead**: shot straight down, for food, products and flat-lays. Also the setup for the [[Object Wipe Transition]]. The "aesthetic flat-lay" look (pastel or warm-amber palette, objects dropped in sequence) is described by OpusClip as "massive on Reels and Pinterest" (vendor claim).
- **Walking POV**: the camera follows at chest height.
- **Off-axis talking**: look just past the lens so it reads as candid. Pairs with the summer-2026 "Netflix documentary" mockumentary format (now fading).

## How to do it
Shoot it in camera. In the edit, only trim and [[Speed Ramp]]. Edits speed curves were listed as upcoming (Meta, 2026-04-22).

## Why it works
It holds attention without visible editing, in line with the [[Lo-Fi Aesthetic]].
