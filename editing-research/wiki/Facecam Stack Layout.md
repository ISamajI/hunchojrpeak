# Facecam Stack Layout

A 9:16 frame with the streamer's camera in the upper third and gameplay or IRL feed below, with captions above the bottom UI zone. It replaces center-cropping 16:9 stream footage.

## How to do it
1. On a 1920×1080 canvas, size the facecam at ~15–20% width.
2. For vertical, place the face in the upper third, keep gameplay central, and keep the lower 15% clear for captions/UI (vendor guidance). Put emotes in a predictable secondary zone.
3. In multi-cam setups, promote the active speaker.
4. Better: set up **Twitch Dual Format** (GA 2026-06-17), which streams separate custom vertical and horizontal layouts at once so every clip and VOD exists natively in both. Twitch also auto-generates portrait clips that "keep your cam and gameplay in frame".

## Why it works
A plain center crop loses the face or the HUD, which is "the reason the moment was good". Twitch says ~70% of new viewers arrive on mobile (Twitch ANZ post).
