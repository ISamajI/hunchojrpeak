# Pinterest

Platform hub. Pinterest video is search-and-save driven: the first frame decides the save. Official guidance on video editing style is thin beyond "payoff first + text overlay"; pacing claims are inference from Pinterest Predicts.

## Techniques
- **Peak**: [[Payoff-First Hook]] (official guidance)
- **Rising**: [[Aesthetic Slow Edits]] (Predicts 2026 palettes; video-style link is inference) · [[Template Remix]] (remixable collages; collages saved at 2x the rate of other Pins, per Pinterest)
- Related: [[Loop Edit]] (end on the result again) · [[Camera POV Reveals]] (flat-lays)

## Key 2026 facts
- Pinterest Create (2026-04-28): "Try opening with the payoff—your reveal, result or key tip—and consider text overlay."
- Pinterest Predicts 2026: Gen Z trading "viral chaos for slower, highly curated lives" (Campaign Asia excerpt); trends growing "4.4x faster".
- Specs: 2:3 (1000×1500) or 9:16; >2:3 may be cropped in feed (verified); overlays ≤10 words (ad spec); keep top/bottom ~15% clear on 9:16 and avoid the bottom ~20% on standard Pins (vendor) — see [[Safe Zones]].
- [[Opus Clip]] added direct publishing to Pinterest (changelog, date not captured).
