# Rehook

Following every resolved point immediately with a new open loop ("but there's one problem…") so there is never a natural exit point.

## How to do it
- Three rehook types: **question**, **tease** (use sparingly), and **pattern interrupt** (new angle, sudden zoom, location change, a smash cut into a burst, an SFX hit).
- Mark every 2 minutes of the outline as a candidate rehook point; decide in the edit which ones earn a place.
- Put the first deliberate pattern interrupt at about 25–35 s ([[Retention Editing]]).
- Never let a section end on a flat pause. In list videos, don't show the count for the last item.

## Why it works
It resets attention at the points where retention graphs typically sag.
