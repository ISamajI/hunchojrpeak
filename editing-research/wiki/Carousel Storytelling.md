# Carousel Storytelling

Swipeable photo (or mixed photo-and-video) posts whose slides and captions tell a story one swipe at a time, including panoramas that "bleed" across slides.

## How to do it
- **TikTok Photo Mode**: a hook line on slide 1, 2–4 supporting images, short captions, and a trending sound. Tools added: Collage (Jan 2026), desktop photo posts and voice typing for photo posts (Jul 2026).
- **[[Edits]] carousel editor (iOS, launched 2026-10-07)**: Projects → **+** → Carousel, add photos and videos, edit with filters, color, text, cutouts and audio, then post directly to Instagram. Android was "coming soon" as of Oct 2026.
  - Conflicting report: one creator blog says the editor ships switched off under Profile → Labs → "Carousel editor". This may vary by account or region.

## Why it works
Each swipe reveals more, and creators can reuse photos they already have. Instagram re-shows carousels to returning followers starting from slide 2 (Social Media Today). New Engen says carousels lead on saves and shares.

## Caveat: reach
Metricool (May 2026, 2.3M posts) found TikTok image/carousel posts up about 140% year over year, but videos still get **5x the reach and 6x the interactions**. Volume is rising faster than performance.

## Examples (TikTok 2026)
"Stop the Wedding, but…" (Nov), the "Actually…" correction carousel (Oct), "Hiding myself to see if anyone will notice" (Aug).
