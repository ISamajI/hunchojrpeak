# Captions

AI editor for talking-head videos and avatars ("AI Twin"). The company and its models are now branded Mirage.

## What creators use it for
Dozens of named AI Edit styles (Prism, Bloom, Pop, Orbit, Bitmap, Scrapbook, Neon and more) that add zooms, B-roll, SFX and transitions automatically; 75+ animated caption styles incl. "Negative Captions" ([[Kinetic Captions]]); eye-contact correction; viral SFX ([[SFX Layering]]); [[AI Dubbing]]; avatar / AI-twin videos.

## 2026 updates (official "What's new")
Clips Chat; Avatar Looks; AI Twin from a video upload or a single photo; rebuilt rendering engine; Horizontal AI Edit; Multi-clip AI Edit; inline translate, transcribe and dub; SRT export; Co-Editor (chat-to-edit, [[Conversational AI Editing]]); Mirage Edit on web; Mirage Avatar X model (blog, 2026-07-28). App Store lists dubbing into 29 languages and subtitles in 91+ languages.

## Price / platforms
Freemium; free plans can now export with a watermark; paid credits for AI features (prices not verified). iOS, web, macOS (returned); legacy desktop signups disabled.
