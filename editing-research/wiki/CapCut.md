# CapCut

ByteDance's all-in-one editor (iOS, Android, desktop, web) and the main editor behind TikTok trends: templates, speed curves, beat markers, auto captions, effects and, since 2026, generative video through Seedance.

## What creators use it for
- [[Template Remix]] ("CapCut – Try this template" links on TikTok). Get templates only from the official app or capcut.com.
- [[Speed Ramp]] (Speed > Curve > Custom; Auto Velocity), [[Beat Sync]] (beat markers), [[Kinetic Captions]] (Auto/AI Captions), [[Green Screen Cutout]] (Remove Background, Chroma Key), [[Split Screen]] overlays, [[Zoom Punch-In]] keyframes.
- [[AI Video Generation]] and [[AI B-Roll]] via [[Seedance]] 2.0. CapCut says Seedance supports "rhythmic editing, with scene cuts that align well with music beats" (vendor).

## 2026 updates
- **Dreamina Seedance 2.0** (TechCrunch 2026-03-26; CapCut Newsroom 2026-04-09): text, image or reference-video to video with synced audio; up to 15 s in 6 aspect ratios; can edit uploaded footage. Rolled out first to paid users in Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Brazil and Mexico, then Africa, South America, Europe, the Middle East, Japan and the US (2026-03-25 to 2026-04-07). Safeguards: initially no generation from real faces; visible and invisible watermarks; C2PA Content Credentials.
- **Video Studio**, a "timeline-free creative space" supporting Seedance 2.0 (No Film School, 2026-03-26).
- Native Android tablet / iPad app (Jun 2026); ChatGPT plugin "coming soon" (Nov 2026); "Edit Pilot" / "AI Agent Editing" tested (Nov 2025) (via SocialBee).

## Price
Freemium. Free plan has no time limit. Pro listed at $19.99/month or $179.99/year (USD) and a $9.99 "Standard" tier appears in-app (**secondary**, via Dupple 2026-10-10; app-store pricing may differ).

## US status
The TikTok USDS Joint Venture announcement says its safeguards "will also cover CapCut, and Lemon8". Deal close 2026-01-22 with ByteDance keeping 19.9% (**secondary**: Wikipedia / BBC). CapCut is live in US app stores per a lower-quality blog. YouTube note advises checking regional availability and terms.
