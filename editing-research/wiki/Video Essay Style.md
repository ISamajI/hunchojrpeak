# Video Essay Style

Vox-style information density: lower thirds, maps, archival footage, one sans-serif font, a chart or map every 8–10 s, music low and voiceover in front. Includes text emphasis (circling, underlining or recoloring a word as it is spoken).

## How to do it
After Effects or Premiere MOGRTs for lower thirds and maps. Keep one typographic system and time graphics to the voiceover. [[Opus Clip]] 4.0 Motion Graphics turns spoken numbers and ideas into animated icons and charts (vendor).

## Why it works
Slower pace but very high watch time when the topic resonates; viewers come away feeling smarter.

## Platforms
[[YouTube]] (peak on long-form, showing up in Shorts). A polished "creator-explainer" Reels style (J-cuts, styled subtitles, UI pop-ups, 3D cursor scenes) was seen in a single pre-window YouTube tutorial; status unknown.
