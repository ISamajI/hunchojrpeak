# Rapid Fire Photo Dump

A day, trip or season crammed into a flash-cut visual diary of very short clips or stills, each held for only a few frames on the beat.

## How to do it
1. Import 20–40 stills or clips from the camera roll.
2. Use beat-detect / auto-beat in [[Edits]] or [[CapCut]], or cut manually so each still sits for 2–6 frames on beats ([[Beat Sync]]).
3. Add a title card at the open.
4. Keep one color filter across all clips for cohesion.

## Why it works
Low effort (camera-roll footage), dense and rewatchable. It is the Reels version of the photo-dump carousel ([[Carousel Storytelling]]).

## Examples (2026, Later)
"Rapid-fire recap" (2026-09-11); "Refuse to be limited by a single niche" (statement cards intercut with photo dumps, 2026-10-23); "Tell me more about yourself" (2026-07-03); "Where are you now" travel dump (2026-07-24).
