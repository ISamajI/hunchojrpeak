# Final Cut Pro

Apple's Mac and iPad pro editor, now also bundled in the Apple Creator Studio subscription, with Final Cut Camera and Live Multicam (up to 4 iPhones or iPads).

## What creators use it for
Multi-iPhone music videos and podcasts; editing iPhone Cinematic-mode footage (change focus after the shot); MotionVFX-style titles, transitions and tracking; transcript-based captions ([[Kinetic Captions]]); heavier grades for [[Phone-First Cinematic]] (OpusClip's recommendation).

## 2026 updates
- **Apple Newsroom, 2026-10-27**: built-in MotionVFX effects, transitions, templates, rotoscoping, tracking and grading; video assets in Content Hub; text-to-image generation in projects; Pixelmator Pro round-trip; more keyframe easing; disconnecting clips from the primary storyline; 23 new transcription languages; Compressor support for Cinematic video from iPhone 18 Pro and "iPhone Duo".
- **Final Cut for iPad 3.4**: Cinematic-mode editing and a Liquid Glass UI.

## Price / platforms
Apple Creator Studio $12.99/month or $129/year (US); education $2.99/month or $29.99/year. Final Cut Pro one-time purchase $299.99 (US). macOS and iPadOS.
