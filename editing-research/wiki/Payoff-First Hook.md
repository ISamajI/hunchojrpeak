# Payoff-First Hook

Opening the video on the finished result (the outfit, the room, the dish), then showing the process, with a short text overlay clarifying the result.

## How to do it
1. Put the final shot as frame 1.
2. Follow with process B-roll.
3. End on the result again (this also makes it loop, see [[Loop Edit]]).
4. Keep overlays to **≤10 words** (Pinterest ad spec guidance), centered and inside [[Safe Zones]].

## Why it works
Pinterest is search-and-save intent; users decide whether to save from the first frame. Pinterest's own creator guidance: "Try opening with the payoff—your reveal, result or key tip—and consider text overlay." **[verified, official]**

## Platforms
[[Pinterest]]. It inverts the end-loaded [[Before And After Reveal]] used on TikTok/Reels.
