# Jump Cut

Cutting pauses, filler words and dead air out of talking-head footage so it plays as a string of quick cuts from one angle.

## How to do it
- Automated: TikTok Studio Smart Split, [[CapCut]], [[Descript]] (text-based editing removes filler words and gaps), Gling (secondary source), [[Submagic]] silence removal.
- Manual: cut on breaths and pauses. On long-form, keep jump cuts for dead air but hold A-roll longer when the message carries itself, and add micro-pauses after big statements (see [[Variable Pacing]]).

## Why it works / where it is going
It removes boredom from single-camera talking heads. But the 2026 sources lean toward **natural pacing** over aggressive dead-air removal (Hootsuite), and AVS4YOU describes "keeping natural pauses and quick cuts" together. AIR names stacked jump cuts as part of over-editing for older audiences. The maximal end survives in [[Brainrot Stack]] (cuts every 0.6–0.8 s).
