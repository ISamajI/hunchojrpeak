# Object Mask

AI subject isolation: selecting one person or object so an effect (blur, highlight, sparkle, selective color, text behind the subject, a tracked tag) applies only to it, or so the background can be removed.

## How to do it
- **[[Edits]]**: Effects → segmentation / "pop" effects, then tap the object (Meta's Segment Anything model). "11 new effects to make objects pop" (@creators on Threads, date unverified). The feature launched around Dec 2025 (pre-window).
- **[[Adobe Premiere Pro]]**: AI Object Mask (v26.0, Jan 2026); shape masks track up to 20x faster.
- **[[Premiere Mobile]]**: one-tap background removal (July 2026).
- **[[Runway]]**: BiRefNet v2 background removal (2026-10-15).
- **[[DaVinci Resolve]] Studio**: Neural Engine region tracking.
- **[[CapCut]]**: Remove Background (see [[Green Screen Cutout]]).
- **Blur tap-to-reveal** (Instagram, fading): blur the whole frame, or one object, and keyframe the blur off at the reveal beat.

## Why it works
It gives VFX-style emphasis (text-behind-subject, product pop) without rotoscoping.
