# Fan Edits

Fan-made music edits of celebrities, athletes and anime built from [[Speed Ramp]]s (velocity), beat-synced cuts, flash/shake transitions and heavy VFX, posted and argued over in stan communities.

## How to do it
1. Pick a 10–20 s audio segment with clear beat drops.
2. Cut on beats ([[Beat Sync]]) and ramp speed (slow-mo into fast) on key motion.
3. Add a flash or shake on drops and a high-contrast color grade.
- After Effects is the high end; [[CapCut]] the low end.

## Why it works
Fandom identity and reshare culture: edits act as "currency" in stan communities, and stan pages are now treated as quasi-news sources (Paper Magazine; Fast Company on stan-to-staff career pipelines).

## Platforms
[[X]] (Stan Twitter is the community hub) · [[TikTok]] · [[Instagram Reels]]

## Status
Peak / saturating. Creators complain that "many edits look the same: repeated transitions." Evidence for X specifically is cultural, not metric **[unverified]**.
