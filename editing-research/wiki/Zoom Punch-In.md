# Zoom Punch-In

Digital 10–20% punch-ins on emphasis words in a single-camera talking head, plus slow continuous zooms or drifts so the frame is never fully still while an idea develops.

## How to do it
- **[[Adobe Premiere Pro]]**: Effect Controls > Scale keyframes (100% → 110–120%), or split the clip and scale the second half for an instant punch.
- **[[DaVinci Resolve]]**: Inspector > Zoom, or Dynamic Zoom.
- **[[CapCut]]**: Animation > "Zoom in", or keyframes.
- Cut on the emphasis word. If two adjacent clips both zoom, match speed and direction.
- **Instagram "small text zoom"** (Later, 2026-10-30, rising): place tiny text in frame, then keyframe a punch-in (or Edits' "zoom" cinematic effect) on the beat so it becomes readable.

## Why it works
Visual variety from one camera angle; movement signals "something is happening". A slow zoom in builds tension; a slow zoom out releases it or reads as an ending.

## Platforms
[[YouTube]] · [[Instagram Reels]] · [[TikTok]]

## Status
Peak, with fatigue when overused. AIR and Pixflow both warn that audiences over 35 retain worse with a zoom on every sentence. The YouTube note expects slow, subtle zooms to outlast hard punches (agent judgement).
