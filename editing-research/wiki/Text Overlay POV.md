# Text Overlay POV

One mostly static shot (a lip-sync, a deadpan face, or a carefree activity) with on-screen text carrying the joke or the relatable point.

## How to do it
1. Film one take.
2. Add a native TikTok text box, keep it inside the center safe area ([[Safe Zones]]), and time it to the audio's punchline.
3. Use trending audio. Business accounts should check that it is in the Commercial Music Library.
- Be specific. New Engen's example: "send an email without the attachment" beats a generic work joke.

## Why it works
It costs almost nothing to make, fits any niche and works on mute. TikTok's own discovery summary for October 2026 names "text-led posts" as one of four main clusters (seen only as a search snippet).

## Examples (2026)
"Why Would I Deceive You?" (Nov 2026), "Go, Go, Go" (Nov 2026), "-A text" (Pretty Little Liars, Oct 2026), "I just want to be OK" (Oct 2026, happy footage under a caption naming an ignored stress).
