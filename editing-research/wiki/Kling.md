# Kling

Kuaishou's image-to-video and text-to-video model, known for strong human motion; native audio and lip-synced dialogue in v3 (audio detail from a secondary source).

## What creators use it for
Animating still photos (old photos, memes, product shots) ([[AI Video Generation]]); multi-shot AI mini-stories ([[Episodic Series]]); consistent recurring characters via "Elements"; [[AI B-Roll]] inside [[Adobe Premiere Pro]] (partner model).

## 2026 updates
Kling AI 3.0 launched 2026-02-05: Video 3.0, Video 3.0 Omni, Image 3.0 and Image 3.0 Omni. Omni adds reference-based consistency (Elements) and a multi-shot storyboard (set duration, shot size, perspective and camera move per shot). Kuaishou says Kling has produced over 600M videos (company claim).

## Price / platforms
Freemium, credit-based (2026 tiers not verified). Web, mobile apps, API, and in Premiere.
