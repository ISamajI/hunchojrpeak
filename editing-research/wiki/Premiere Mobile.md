# Premiere Mobile

Adobe Premiere for iPhone (since Sept 2025) and Android (since 2026-09-22): a free mobile NLE that is Adobe's answer to CapCut. Adobe Premiere Rush reached end of life on 2026-09-30.

## What creators use it for
[[Speed Ramp]] (Speed Curves), [[Beat Sync]] (Beat Detection), [[Long-to-Short Clipping]] (one-tap Auto Reframe 16:9 → 9:16), [[Object Mask]] (one-tap background removal), [[Kinetic Captions]] (animated captions), [[Phone-First Cinematic]] (.cube LUT import), Firefly visuals and voice-to-SFX.

## 2026 release notes
- April: keyframes, .cube LUT import, Beat Detection, crop presets.
- May: Auto Reframe.
- June: Speed Curves, audio keyframes, one-tap stabilization.
- July: one-tap background removal, redesigned toolbar, voiceover updates.
- August: Adobe Fresco animation import.
- September: Android launch (Android 13+, at least 5GB RAM).
- October: Adobe Fonts.

## Price / platforms
Free app; generative AI uses Firefly credits (limits not verified). iPhone, Android.
