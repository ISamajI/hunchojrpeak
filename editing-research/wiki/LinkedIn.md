# LinkedIn

Platform hub. LinkedIn video in 2026 favours vertical, expert-POV talking heads with burned-in captions and repurposed podcast/webinar clips. Much of the tactical guidance comes from vendors (Visla, OpusClip, Contentin.io, Blitzcut).

## Techniques
- **Rising**: [[Talking Head Video]] (expert POV, captioned) · [[Native Vertical Video]] (contested: vendor says vertical boosted; one creator reports horizontal ~10x better at ~2 min)
- **Peak**: [[Long-to-Short Clipping]] (podcast/webinar repurposing; "reframe, don't repost")
- Supporting: [[Kinetic Captions]] (≤42 chars/line, lower third above buttons; vendor)

## Key 2026 facts
- LinkedIn Creator Video newsletter (May 2026): best videos are "grounded in real experience and a clear point of view"; 2–5 posts/week, 2 of them video.
- Immersive video tab/carousel re-expanded to CA/UK/AU (May 2026); paid video up ~30% YoY in Q1 2026.
- "AI slop" report button (cross-platform anti-AI-slop trend).
- Wistia/HubSpot (via Rework, vendor-cited secondary): 67% of teams that repurpose post clips to LinkedIn first.
- Native video avoids the outbound-link reach penalty; link-out posts are fading.
- Specs: 9:16 for 30–90 s discovery; 16:9 / 4:5 for 2–5 min education; organic up to 10 min (Sprout) — see [[Safe Zones]].
