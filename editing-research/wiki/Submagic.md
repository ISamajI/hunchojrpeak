# Submagic

Web tool for fast animated captions plus auto B-roll, zooms, silence removal and "Magic Clips" for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

## What creators use it for
Hormozi/MrBeast-style bold word-by-word captions with emojis and highlight colors ([[Kinetic Captions]]; 35+ styles per a vendor comparison; Hormozi preset uses Anton font with yellow stroke), auto zoom-cuts ([[Zoom Punch-In]]), [[Brainrot Stack]] captions, [[Long-to-Short Clipping]] (Magic Clips).

## Price
Paid, per member (official help center): Starter $19/month (45 credits), Pro $39/month (120), Business $69/month (300); annual billing saves up to 41%; Magic Clips cost 8 credits each. Web.
