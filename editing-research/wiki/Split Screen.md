# Split Screen

Several clips shown at once (stacked vertically, side by side, or as panels) or in quick sequence for an instant comparison or a multi-panel collage.

## How to do it
- **[[CapCut]]**: add an Overlay for each clip, scale it to 1/3 height and stack.
- **TikTok editor**: photo/video overlays for picture-in-picture (TikTok Newsroom post is undated and probably pre-2025, so possibly stale).
- **[[Edits]]**: overlays, scale and crop, or a layout/collage template. Sync the panel changes to the beat.
- **YouTube long-form**: picture-in-picture for tutorials (AIR cites Ali Abdaal).

## Why it works
The comparison is instant to read and the format is easy to remix.

## Examples (2026)
- TikTok: "Boy, man, kebab" (Oct 2026, three stacked videos); "Aura, Laura" (Aug 2026, doing it well vs failing); "Dame Hombro" (Nov 2026, "me vs. AI" side-by-side).
- Instagram: three-panel "what I ate / what I wore / where I went" (Later, 2026-07-24); "Send me a photo every hour" side-by-side timelines (2026-08-14).
