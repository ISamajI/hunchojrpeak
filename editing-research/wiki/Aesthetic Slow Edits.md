# Aesthetic Slow Edits

Slow-paced, calm video with ambient or ASMR audio, a cohesive color palette and soft transitions, themed on Pinterest Predicts 2026 aesthetics.

## How to do it
1. Pick one Predicts palette and grade all clips to it.
2. Use 1–2 s holds with gentle crossfades or [[Match Cut]]s.
3. Keep text minimal and let visuals carry it (B-roll beats talking head for aesthetic niches, per a vendor comparison).
- Predicts 2026 themes: Cool Blue ("glacier aesthetic +35%"), Vamp Romantic, Neo Deco, Glamoratti, Glitchy Glam, Gimme Gummy, Mystic Outlands, Pen Pals, Brooched, Cabbage Crush.

## Why it works
Pinterest Predicts 2026 frames Gen Z as trading "viral chaos for slower, highly curated lives" (Campaign Asia excerpt). Pinterest says trends are growing "4.4x faster".
