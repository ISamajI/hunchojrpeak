## Cross-platform patterns

1. **Raw beats polished, but maximalism survives in pockets.** The [[Over-Editing Backlash]] shows up on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube long-form, Pinterest and Snapchat. The counter-signal is the [[Brainrot Stack]], which OpusClip (a vendor) calls 2026's "dominant short-form aesthetic". The YouTube note concludes both extremes perform on Shorts; the backlash is clearest on long-form and with older audiences.
2. **Originality is now enforced by distribution.** YouTube (2026-10-01), X (Kickstart / Original Content Rewards), Snapchat (2026-04-02 and 2026-07-31) and the clip economy's own warnings all point the same way: add commentary or real editing, and don't repost. See [[Transformative Editing]] and [[Cross-Posting Hygiene]].
3. **AI editing is accepted; fully AI-generated posts are not.** Generation now sits inside [[CapCut]] ([[Seedance]]), [[Adobe Premiere Pro]] (Firefly + Veo/Kling/Runway/Luma), YouTube ([[Veo and Gemini Omni]]) and [[Edits]]. At the same time Snapchat stopped recommending fully AI-generated Spotlights, LinkedIn added an "AI slop" report button, and Hootsuite reports consumer wariness of AI ads. See [[AI Editing Shift 2026]] and [[AI Content Labeling]].
4. **Templates are the distribution mechanic.** CapCut templates on TikTok, Edits template projects on Reels, Snap Quick Cut remix and Pinterest remixable collages all lower the cost of joining a trend ([[Template Remix]]).
5. **Vertical-first is now universal, with designed (not cropped) layouts.** X and LinkedIn joined the 9:16 push; Twitch ships native vertical layouts. Captions must sit inside platform [[Safe Zones]].
6. **The hook is becoming measurable.** YouTube Studio's A/B testing of up to 3 cuts (announced, "coming soon") and Edits' multiple reel versions let creators test openings ([[Hook AB Testing]]).
7. **Captions and translation converge.** Kinetic captions are standard; bilingual captions (Edits, 15 languages) and built-in auto-dubbing (YouTube 27 languages; Meta AI Translations) are the new layer ([[AI Dubbing]]).

## What's fading

- **Uniform, ultra-fast "overstim" cutting on long-form**, plus a zoom and whoosh on every sentence ([[Variable Pacing]], [[Zoom Punch-In]]). Source data on MrBeast is from 2024; 2026 agency guidance agrees.
- **Aggregator re-uploads and syndicated reposts** on YouTube Shorts, Snapchat and X ([[Transformative Editing]]).
- **Fully AI-generated posts** off X: Snapchat ineligible since 2026-07-31 ([[AI Video Generation]]).
- **Watermarked cross-posts** and **center-cropped 16:9 stream clips** ([[Cross-Posting Hygiene]], [[Facecam Stack Layout]]).
- **Link-out posts on LinkedIn** (native video avoids the penalty).
- **Long branded intros / "hey guys" openers** on YouTube.
- **Summer-2026 Instagram formats**: black-and-white-to-color reveal, flicker edit, blur tap-to-reveal, "Netflix documentary" mockumentary (see [[Instagram Reels]]).
- **Possibly fading** (agent inference, no source states it): [[Kinetic Captions]] as a *differentiator*; [[Jump Cut]] pacing as a deliberate style.
- **[[Sora]]**: gone.

## Caveats and confidence

**Overall confidence:** medium for direction, low for magnitude. No source measured technique usage or views per technique.

### Claims resting on vendor or agency blogs (treat as directional)
- **OpusClip** (sells clipping/caption tools): brainrot as "the dominant short-form aesthetic of 2026"; talking-head static "built dozens of $1M-ARR creators"; captions as "the most important retention mechanism"; the aesthetics list behind [[Phone-First Cinematic]], [[Brainrot Stack]], [[Talking Head Video]].
- **AIR Media-Tech** (sells audits; Epidemic Sound partner): the 100-channel pacing analysis, audience calibration numbers, SFX dB/BPM levels ([[Variable Pacing]], [[SFX Layering]]).
- **Pixflow** (vendor): retention editing guidance and vidIQ drop-off figure.
- **Caption-tool vendors** (ReframeX, Reap, Choppity, Exemplary AI): caption styles and lift. Exemplary AI itself found **no controlled study** showing Hormozi captions increase views.
- **LinkedIn tactics** (Visla, OpusClip, Contentin.io, Blitzcut): vertical boost, 65% vs 5% feed share, 40–60% caption watch-time lift.
- **Snapchat watermark suppression** (ShortSync) and **Pinterest/Snap safe-zone pixel guidance from agencies** (Spilno, TrymyPost).
- **CreatorDB** (data vendor): clip-economy census.
- **Hootsuite, BrandLens, AVS4YOU, beCreatives**: the lo-fi / over-editing direction (repeated consensus, not measured).
- **Platform self-reported figures**: Meta's Edits save/reshare uplift; YouTube's 72% AI-use stat and Omni usage; Snap's poster growth; Kling's 600M videos; Pinterest's collage 2x save rate.

### Contradictions between raw notes
1. **Edits desktop**: TikTok note cites a Nov 2026 "Edits launches for MacOS" headline (homepage only); tools note found no shipping confirmation.
2. **Edits AI assistant**: tools note says "in testing" (Jun 2026 source); Instagram note says rolled out to all US users ~2026-09-30 (Net Influencer). The later report likely supersedes.
3. **Kinetic captions direction**: peak/saturated (TikTok), peak/staple (Instagram), "fading as a signature look" (YouTube, inference), rising (X composer).
4. **AI video generation status**: rising with backlash (TikTok), rising (Instagram), "peak on X / fading elsewhere" for fully-AI content (Other Platforms).
5. **Seedance 2.0 in CapCut date**: TechCrunch 2026-03-26 (TikTok note) vs CapCut Newsroom 2026-04-09 with rollout 2026-03-25 to 04-07 (tools note). These are consistent once read as report date vs newsroom post date.
6. **Seedance 2.5**: TikTok note says it powers Symphony AI video ads (Aug 2026, via SocialBee); tools note could confirm it only in Runway's changelog (2026-10-02) and not in CapCut.
7. **Premiere Pro versions / pricing**: YouTube note credits single-word captions to 26.3 (retailer blog) and says AI features are in the standard subscription (Rework excerpt); tools note documents the Sept 2026 features as v26.5 and says generative features use Firefly credits.
8. **Maximal vs minimal**: Hootsuite/New Engen/AIR say over-editing is out; OpusClip calls brainrot the dominant 2026 aesthetic.
9. **Jump cuts**: TikTok note calls them mature/possibly fading; YouTube note keeps them for dead-air removal; Instagram/YouTube brainrot notes use aggressive jump cuts.
10. **Ideal Reels length** (within the Instagram note): 6–15 s loops for trends vs 30–60 s highest reach (Socialinsider via CreatorFlow).
11. **LinkedIn vertical vs horizontal** (within the Other Platforms note): vendors say vertical is boosted; a creator anecdote says horizontal won ~10x at ~2 min.
